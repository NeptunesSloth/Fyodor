# Optional private library inspection

Planar Atlas, Fertile Thicket, Cream of the Crop, Quest for Ula's Temple, and Nissa, Steward of Elements compile to the existing optional-ability, exact look/pick, and library-order continuations. Atlas and Thicket can decline either the initial look or selection of a qualifying land. Declining to select a land puts every looked-at card on the bottom. Atlas randomizes that bottom packet; Thicket lets the controller order it privately. The chosen land is revealed during the ordering and then hidden again when resolution ends, using its exact original library object.

Cream reads the entering creature's power when its trigger resolves, including response effects or last known information after the creature leaves. Negative power is treated as zero. A positive look requires keeping one card if available; its remaining packet is privately ordered on the bottom. The enchantment's later departure does not stop its trigger.

Quest makes two separate choices: whether to look, then whether to reveal a qualifying creature card and add a quest counter. A private filtered card decision is still offered with no options when a noncreature was looked at, so other players cannot infer the top card's type from the presence of a prompt. The reveal is cleaned up even if counters are prohibited or the source has left; the exact source check prevents counters on a new incarnation. Ordinary native counter replacements remain in force.

Nissa is actually cast with X and receives native loyalty counters. Her zero ability reads current loyalty when resolving, or last known loyalty if she left in response. A land is eligible independently of that bound; a creature card must have mana value at most the bound. The card remains on top if declined, ineligible, or unable to enter. Native permanent entry and exact library guards handle replacements and cards that left and returned. Modal double-faced cards are inspected using the front face in the library, rather than treating a back-face land as a land card.

Native look filters now use current derived characteristics through Plan.ch, including in the multi-kind adapter. This fixes Grist being omitted from creature-card choices outside the battlefield while preserving ordinary planeswalker exclusions and front-face rules. The change affects qualification only, not the inspected object set or visibility.

Official rulings consulted: Fertile Thicket (2015), Cream of the Crop (2008), Quest for Ula's Temple (2010), and Nissa (2017). These explicitly establish Thicket's no-keep option, Cream's resolution/LKI power and zero-power behavior, Quest's independent choices and hidden top card, and Nissa's last-known-loyalty rule. Canonical fixtures preserve complete DB metadata. Gameplay tests cover responses, source departure, replacement counters, saved private choices and ordering, and HTTP/WebSocket visibility before the look, during a public selected-card reveal, and after cleanup.
