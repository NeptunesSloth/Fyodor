# Volatile Stormdrake: ability-source hexproof and exchange retention

Official rulings: https://api.scryfall.com/cards/2e6e3232-8bb8-4504-9597-dfdfc6d634bd/rulings . The drake must remain its original battlefield object and the target must remain legal; otherwise the exchange and all subsequent instructions do nothing. The exchange lasts indefinitely and does not end if the drake later leaves.

The exact combined hexproof quality “activated and triggered abilities” tests explicit source metadata. targets.source_of assigns is_spell/is_ability mutually exclusively from the targeting object's kind, including when an ability's source itself is a spell on the stack (Ulamog's cast trigger) or is read from last-known information. as_spell explicitly clears the ability flag. Color/type/other source characteristics remain those of the source; existing opponent-only hexproof, hexproof-ignore effects, and losing hexproof still apply. The grammar deliberately does not claim activated-only or triggered-only subqualities without actual subtype information.

A typed leaf instruction delegates control exchange to the existing native primitive, then verifies the captured exact OIDs and swapped current controllers. Only a completed exchange queues native energy gain, native optional energy payment equal to the acquired creature's current mana value, and sacrifice if unpaid. Energy replacements and paused decisions use normal continuations. Native sacrifice requires the resolving ability's controller still to control the permanent, and sacrifice restrictions remain effective.

If the original ability controller has lost control of the drake to a third player, the two other players can still exchange their creatures. The original controller gets energy and can decline payment, but cannot sacrifice the creature a third player controls. If both objects have the same current controller, nothing is exchanged and no energy is gained. If the drake blinks, its new trigger can exchange the new object; the old trigger cannot exchange that new object or grant extra energy.

Canonical gameplay tests exercise successful payment/decline, unaffordable and zero-value payments (including a token target), Vorinclex energy replacement, target/source departure, current-controller cases, indefinite control, Cloudshift/new-object identity, Assault Suit and own Tajuru Preserver, opponent activated/triggered abilities versus spells, own activated abilities, actual Ulamog cast-trigger targeting, Glaring Spotlight, Arcane Lighthouse, Dress Down, source LKI, and mutually exclusive source flags. Choices and final states round-trip save/load and replay. Fixtures retain real Oracle and canonical metadata.
