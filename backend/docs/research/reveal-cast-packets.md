# Reveal/exile packets and native cast continuations

The batch38 leaf supports sixteen canonical cards by composing native movement, exact object bindings, optional casting, permission durations and entry replacement handling. Canonical metadata and official ruling snapshots were fetched before implementation (`/tmp/reveal-cast38-canonical.json`, `/tmp/reveal-cast38-rulings.json`). No separate casting or selection engine is introduced.

Collision of Realms follows its February 2022 ruling literally: all owners shuffle creatures first, all qualifying owners reveal next, all found creatures enter in one shared entry batch, then the remaining packets go to their respective library bottoms. An incoming Soul Warden controlled by a later player sees the earlier player's incoming creature, proving the entries are simultaneous.

Each reveal/exile packet has its own exact original objects. Possibility Storm still acts when the original spell was countered before its trigger resolves, and its cleanup includes only its own original/exiled objects that remain exiled. Mirror-Mad Phantasm's named card is a fixed literal even when Clone copies the ability. Reweave reads types before the actual sacrifice and rejects Instant/Sorcery cards even if a Kindred type is shared, as its official ruling requires.

Cast permissions have two different forms. Spellshift, Creative Technique and similar instructions cast immediately as the effect resolves; their original library-card filter is not reapplied to other otherwise legal faces of the found card. Kiora and Discover the Impossible explicitly qualify the resulting SPELL, so their filters evaluate its actual face and mana value. Discover can cast Petty Theft from an exiled Brazen Borrower despite the creature front; declined or impossible casts return only the unchanged selected exile object to hand. Its neutral hidden cast offer remains private even when no eligible spell exists.

Territorial Bruntar and Nicol Bolas grant exact-object permissions until end of turn and obey normal timing. The Day of the Doctor grants play permission only while its exact Saga object remains on the battlefield. Its fourth chapter chooses up to three Doctors without targeting or a controller/creature restriction, then optionally exiles other creatures and deals the printed damage. The natural-chapter proof protects an opponent’s Universal Automaton and a third player’s noncreature Firdoch Core (Kindred Artifact with changeling), while exiling the controller’s unchosen Lonis. All four Saga chapters and all four Bolas abilities have actual gameplay proof.

Synthesis Pod uses the ordinary native cost framework. Its exact stack-spell selector rejects abilities and opponents' spells. The chosen spell is exiled while paying, before an ability is created; a frozen paid-card fact records its actual pre-exile face and X-inclusive mana value. The Phyrexian mana decision, cancellation, payment ordering and ability copying stay with existing cost machinery. Other exiled-card cost receipt semantics are preserved.

A genuine shared bounds bug was also fixed: an instruction can publicly reveal a library larger than the table's top-N action limit of 100. The existing native look primitive now uses ordinary public Reveal actions in bounded chunks for those packets, preserving exact receipt construction and private-look behavior. The official Blessed Reincarnation no-match case is tested with more than 100 cards.

## Validation

All sixteen canonical primary cards have actual positive gameplay proof. The final unique total is 49 owned cases plus 120 native reveal, activation-cost, Phyrexian and rebound regressions (169 passed). The natural four-chapter Doctor test was then rerun successfully with both opponent and noncreature Doctor choices. Fresh-engine reloads cover nine distinct points, including multiplayer reveal/cast sequencing, paid stack-spell X receipts, source-independent permissions, private exile, source LKI, copied names and ordinary casting callbacks. The Discover privacy timeline passes both REST and WebSocket projections. All owned app/test Ruff checks pass.
