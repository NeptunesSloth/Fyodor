# Conditional own costs

Ten canonical cards reuse native announced costs rather than recording historical sacrifices or introducing payment state. Visions of Glory, Dominance, Ruin, Duplicity and Dread apply their commander-mana-value reduction only when their own printed flashback alternative is selected. The native maximum value selects owned commanders in the battlefield or command zone, regardless of their controller. Other flashback grants, ordinary casts and free casts retain their own costs. The total is locked before mana abilities are activated (CR 601.2f).

Ice Out, Johann's Stopgap and Hamlet Glutton attach their reductions to the existing announced bargain sacrifice component. Exactly one sacrifice pays that component and earns one reduction; declining bargain pays the full cost. Existing generic floors, cost increases and alternative costs continue through the same payment pipeline. Assassin's Ink and Geistlight Snare sum two independently conditional reductions. An unknown condition refuses the entire compound parser.

Visions of Dread uses the existing mandatory opponent-choice return primitive: the targeted opponent chooses a creature card in their own graveyard, then the caster receives it. This is not a targeted creature card or a caster's selection. Native entry replacements, source-object tracking and saved decisions remain in force.

Canonical gameplay tests cover all ten bodies, printed versus Snapcaster alternatives, a bargain UI offer with only discounted mana, independent condition combinations, enemy-controlled owned commanders versus stolen enemy commanders, source sacrifice during mana payment after the total is locked, replay and a fresh-process Dread choice.
