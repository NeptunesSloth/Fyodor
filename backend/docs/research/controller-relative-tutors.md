# Controller-relative tutors and movement compensation

Canonical Oracle fixtures and all ten expected cards are in `tests/fixtures/controller_tutor_cards.json`; the first ten entries are the new family. The implementation composes native searches, exact object bindings, per-player contexts, partitions, entry replacements, optional payments, and shuffle actions.

Arcum Dagsson and Magmatic Hellkite capture the legal target's controller before attempting its sacrifice or destruction. Their official rulings say that player searches even when the sacrifice or destruction fails. An illegal target instead prevents the whole ability resolving. Hellkite uses the native before-entry stun-counter recipe, including Solemnity.

Rootweaver Druid processes opponents in APNAP order. Each searching opponent chooses one found land for the original trigger controller and puts the rest under their own control; the one-card case is mandatory. Native partition metadata supplies these recipients while preserving Agent authority and entry replacements. Participating libraries shuffle only after every opponent has completed their instructions.

Krenko's Buzzcrusher chooses up to one nonbasic land for each player without targeting, then destroys the chosen lands together. From the Ashes destroys all nonbasics together. Wave of Vitriol sacrifices the artifact/enchantment/nonbasic-land union together, counting a physical permanent once. Exact native movement receipts retain each land's former controller and derived characteristics; prevented destruction or prohibited sacrifices create no entitlement. Each eligible controller's basic-land search is one native packet, preserving search restrictions and a single final shuffle. CR 701.23h treats searching for cards and then other cards in the same instruction as one search.

Natural Balance asks six-plus-land players which five to retain, sacrifices the rest together, then offers eligible low-land players their native basic-land packets. Turtle Tracks preserves its any-number player target group and runs each legal target's optional search in that player's context.

Vizier of the Anointed's query checks current library characteristics for embalm or eternalize. The activation event captures the actual keyword ability from its original or last-known source, after costs are paid. Its ordinary draw trigger uses the stack and resolves before the token-producing ability. Copying an ability does not emit an activation event.

Transmute Artifact sacrifices an artifact as a mandatory resolving instruction, not an additional casting cost or optional sacrifice. Its official rulings distinguish this resolution-time instruction from casting costs. The sacrificed artifact's exact last-known mana value determines the later difference payment. The native payment continuation survives saves; decline puts the found artifact into its owner's graveyard. Native outcome bindings exclude Opposition Agent's diverted finds, so no later payment or return can retrieve them.

Hibernation's End remains outside this family: the generic cumulative-upkeep pipeline needs a successful-payment event emitted after the actual resumable payment finishes. Oath of Lieges also remains separate: its active player chooses targets for another player's triggered ability, and its relative targeting requirement must hold at both announcement and resolution. Neither is treated as automated by this work.

Tests exercise successful native effects for all ten cards, canonical metadata, replay/JSON restoration, fresh-session decisions, destruction and sacrifice prohibitions, Agent authority and permissions, entry-counter prevention, actual target fizzle, and the timing of Vizier's draw. Arcum's foreign-library search is checked through REST and WebSocket adapters for both player and spectator privacy.
