# Coin outcomes and saved native repeats

Batch38 implements twenty canonical cards through one replacement-aware simultaneous coin group and ordinary native consequences. Canonical inputs and official rulings are staged in /tmp/coin-outcomes38-{cards,rulings}.json; accepted names are in /tmp/coin-outcomes38-expected.json.

The group records a replacement tree for each original coin, preserving applied replacement identities on its descendant flips. All physical outcomes of all simultaneous participants are recorded before ignored-result choices. Kept results alone generate win/loss facts. Edgar modifies its player's whole first group to heads and explicit wins, including otherwise heads/tails-only instructions. Actual flipped-turn history is separate from winning history; older missing public history is restored explicitly when retained public logs cannot establish it.

Mandatory repeated flips retain a saved cursor instead of the former 1000-flip completion cap. A Continue decision is a processing checkpoint during the same resolution. It grants no priority and does not perform state-based actions. Mana Clash has a fixed-size repeated instruction program; each round uses one simultaneous coin group and one native simultaneous damage instruction. Its native lethal-round regression reaches -15 life, pauses while the caster remains in the game and the spell remains on the stack, then ends on heads/heads before ordinary state-based loss. The same boundary is verified in a fresh process after a native view.

Fiery Gambit has a genuine choice after every win and can exceed three flips. Crooked Scales pays each retry through ordinary optional mana payment, retaining its original target slots. Breeches uses native reflexive triggers after its own paid artifact cost and kept coin result, preserving the original cast event for the copy or mana-value damage branch. Delayed coin effects and Sliver grants use exact object references and native delayed return/ownership handling.
