# Damage redirection

Fifteen canonical cards reuse native damage replacement records, source choices, exact object references and effect continuations. Actor metadata distinguishes an original Martyrdom grant controller from the creature's later controller; Personal Incarnation uses the actual card owner. Borrowing the granted ability text with Experiment Kraj does not copy the external activation permission.

Finite redirection is opt-in and currently supports one original recipient across multiple simultaneous source packets. A pure replacement plan gathers capacities before any damage, shield consumption or lifelink commits. The affected recipient chooses the split through saved native number decisions; replacement ordering is recalculated and the vector is revalidated against the resulting capacities. Captain's Maneuver, Razia and Hazduhr use this path. Ordinary existing records retain their previous behavior.

Cover of Winter, Harm's Way and Shining Shoal remain excluded: their one budget spans distinct original recipients and needs a separately reviewed chooser/frontier design. Nova Pentacle's opponent chooses a target at announcement; Blood of Martyr has a different optional chooser. Neither gap is approximated by a resolution choice.

Canonical tests exercise all fifteen printed effects, repeated damage, controller changes, departure, printed flashback, zero X, protected destinations, a 1+2 simultaneous allocation, native Furnace ordering, exhausted pools and fresh-process resumes of both combat and resolving-effect decisions. An explicit cross-recipient guard prevents accidental use of the bounded allocator for the excluded cards.
