# Scoped draw replacement sequences

Canonical Oracle fixtures are in `tests/fixtures/draw_replacement_sequence_cards.json`. The implemented thirteen-card family is Urabrask, Heretic Praetor; Archmage Ascension; Uba Mask; Island Sanctuary; Chains of Mephistopheles; Zur's Weirding; Shared Fate; Breathstealer's Crypt; Words of Wind; Plagiarize; Aladdin's Lamp; Words of War; and Ring of Ma'rûf.

The existing native replacement planner owns CR 616 choices, optional application, CR 614.5 ancestry and per-card draw processing. New bodies run under the affected drawer while retaining the replacement's original controller for discard prohibitions. Timed records freeze targets, source objects and announced X when the original ability resolves, rather than retargeting later. Archmage searches remain independent searches/shuffles for each replaced draw. Island Sanctuary's source-independent attack rule lasts until that player's next turn; flying and islandwalk independently qualify, and planeswalker attacks are unaffected.

Breathstealer's Crypt performs its own inner draw, followed only by its own unmodified actual hand object (CR 121.6c). Private `TurnLog.drawn_applied` records final ancestry at actual committed draws. It defaults to `{}` for old saves; absence is unknown, never permission to reveal a guessed card. Follow-up compares exact inherited replacement IDs and excludes count-modified draws. Existing broad `drawn_replaced` and `drawn_counted` flags retain their meaning for outer follow-ups. Neither current nor previous private turn-log maps are included in the explicit player/spectator rules-view whitelist, REST view/history, WebSocket frames or snapshot summaries. Cold/replay and adapter tests cover this boundary.

Shared Fate's face-down exile grants a personal native look right (CR 406.3) and a separate exact-object PLAY permission. LOOK survives source departure; PLAY requires the same source object to still have the derived static ability. Uba permissions also expire at end of turn. Source reentry never restores old permissions. Playing a face-down exiled land requires both the actor's existing LOOK authority and a currently valid native PLAY grant before the copied face-up legality probe is usable. Unauthorized IDs receive the same authorization error regardless of hidden type. The original card remains untouched until its normal play/entry action; lands enter face up and preserve foreign ownership, current entry rules and land-play limits. Face-down battlefield/Manifest objects are unchanged.

Zur's Weirding reveals the exact library object, then offers native life payments in APNAP order to the other players, stopping after the first payment. An empty library still permits payment: paying replaces the draw and prevents empty-library draw loss. Declining continues an actual draw with the Weirding already excluded.

Ring of Ma'rûf invokes the existing outside-game policy without adding a collection fallback. Commander CR 903.11 prevents ordinary traditional outside cards from entering the game: the replaced draw is consumed. Other formats retain the existing outside-game implementation and its limitations. Imported decks do not override format rules.

Scion of Halaster and Reed Richards, Smartest Man remain PARTIAL. First-qualified *would-draw* history cannot be approximated by actual cards drawn when another replacement eliminates all derived draws. Independent rules research and committed-event/source-reentry tests gate those two extensions.

Official card rulings used include Archmage Ascension (2009-10-01: each individual draw is a separate search/shuffle and first ability has an intervening-if), Shared Fate (2004-10-04 and later: replacing an empty-library draw does not cause draw loss; retained look at cards personally exiled), Aladdin's Lamp (2004-10-04: additional Lamps may replace the inner draw), and Urabrask (2022-04-29: normal costs and timing remain required). Full fetched WotC rulings and exact Oracle source data were staged in `/tmp/draw-replacement39-rulings.json` and `/tmp/draw-replacement39-canonical.json` during implementation.
