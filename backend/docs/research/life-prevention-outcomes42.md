# Life/prevention outcomes, batch 42

After batch 41's verified central cut, the exact three recipes in `life_prevention_outcomes42.py` are registered in the production parser, definition and primitive paths. Whole-card and genuine fresh-process tests use production registration without adapters.

Transcendence uses its native zero-or-less-life static and a genuine lose-life event trigger that gains twice the captured loss amount. Damage and nondamage loss both trigger it; this is a triggered gain, not replacement life loss. Its exact 20-or-more-life state trigger reuses the isolated pure-loss proof with explicit trusted step, state text and exact definition callback. All existing Coil guards/default behavior remain. An already-triggered loss has no intervening-if clause: falling below 20 or source departure does not cancel it. The ordinary priority round remains before the actual loss resolution can prove a mandatory draw.

Screaming Nemesis uses native incoming-damage aggregation, genuine triggered targets and exact original source identity for any-other targeting. Its damage step opts into `bind_damage_players`, recording the positive final native DamageOutcome player recipients before the saved terminal. The normal context binding persists through damage replacement decisions and immediate rider continuations. The following native `cant_gain_life` instruction applies for the rest of the game to the actual players damaged, including redirected or split players. Prevented-zero and creature-only damage create no player lock. A dead or returned source retains the original trigger's source OID/LKI.

Flames of the Blood Hand binds its original legal player or planeswalker's current controller before its unpreventable damage. After damage it installs an ordinary native life replacement with an exact player-list match and `set 0`, owned by the original caster through the current turn. Redirection does not change that bound player. A planeswalker dying during damage does not erase its previously captured controller. This is deliberately separate from the prohibition primitive: the affected player can order this replacement against Tainted Remedy through normal 616, choosing zero gain or replacement life loss. A prohibition would suppress that choice incorrectly.

Shared prerequisites are bounded: an opt-in damage binding; exact player-list matching in native life replacement applicability, with legacy matches unchanged; alpha-renaming for the new binding; and explicit recipe parameters on the existing pure-loss proof. No generic state-trigger grammar, generic loss implementation, global damage allocator or mandatory-loop cap changes.
