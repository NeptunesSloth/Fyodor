# Qualified library selections and native continuations

Batch37 admits 24 canonical cards from the qualified-selection fixture. The leaf composes the native exact-object look/pick/rest queue, payment receipts, actual battlefield arrival records, normal counterable reflexive triggers, grants and private ordering. It does not create an alternate selection or movement engine. Canonical metadata and official rulings were read from the Oracle database and Scryfall ruling records before implementation; preparation snapshots are /tmp/qualified-look37-canonical.json and /tmp/qualified-look37-rulings.json.

Owner, revealer, chooser and battlefield controller remain separate. Lonis and Balshan Beguiler have the affected opponent reveal their own library, then restore the original ability controller for the actual choice. Ashnod's Cylix gives its target the private look and choice. Temporary public rights are removed from cards remaining in libraries, including a single selected Munda card whose native top placement has no ordering decision.

All mana-value filters use the native selector comparison `mv`, not an invented field. Woodwurm freezes its resolution-time power for both look count and mana-value cap. Eos reads immutable actual convoke contributions. Debut reads the exact spell's additional-cost decision, which native copies retain. Actual mana spent is a different receipt and is not inferred from these cost decisions.

Entry instructions run through native replacement/prohibition handling. Follow-ups bind actual arrivals; a prohibited original still in the library belongs to the remaining packet, while a redirected new exile object does not. Invocation and Gilgamesh create ordinary reflexive stack abilities after their printed first instructions. Touch grants its recurring end-step ability to the creature itself, so current controller and exact creature incarnation govern it.

## Deliberately unresolved card

Zimone's Experiment remains PARTIAL and the parser explicitly declines its recipe. A land creature satisfies both final instructions. Written-order interpretation under CR608.2c and the public-movement exception CR400.7j suggests battlefield entry followed by the actual new object's movement to hand. However, CR400.6 also addresses mutually exclusive destinations, and no official card ruling settles whether these are separate movement events or how a replacement-diverted public exile should participate in the second instruction. No global exact-reference relaxation or guessed source identity was introduced. The other 24 cards are independent of that unresolved overlap.

## Validation

Final focused run: 125 passed, one deliberately excluded Zimone case skipped. This includes 24 canonical classifications, positive actual gameplay for all 24, thirteen cold/replacement/privacy/alternative-branch cases and a real Double Major copy of a convoked Knight-Errant of Eos, plus 63 native library-look/convoke/distribution/attached-look regressions. All chosen actions are replayed through persisted native intents. Fresh-engine reloads cover entry replacement, opponent exile-packet choices, optional sacrifices, recurring granted end-step abilities and copied contribution receipts. Bamboozle also passes actual REST and WebSocket public-reveal to private-order projections. Owned Ruff and Python compilation pass.
