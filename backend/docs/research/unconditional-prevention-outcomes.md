# Unconditional additional prevention outcomes

Angel of Suffering, Gatta and Luzzu, and Sokrates, Athenian Teacher use their full Oracle definitions. Their official rulings explicitly require the mill, counters, or draw even when damage cannot be prevented. These additional instructions use the attempted damage amount, after earlier replacements, rather than the amount prevented.

Angel supplies a native static full-prevention candidate with an unconditional immediate mill body. Gatta's actual enters trigger announces a creature controlled by the trigger controller and installs an end-of-turn full-prevention record bound to its original target object. Its native `consume_unpreventable` flag preserves the additional counter body even when prevention is zero. Sokrates grants the actual quoted static ability through native layer 6; later ability loss removes it, a later grant restores it, and expiry and source departure follow the native continuous effect. The draw instruction names the creature's controller and damaged player together and uses the native simultaneous-player draw machinery.

The existing damage rider context retains both actual prevention and attempted damage. Counter, draw, mill, replacement-order and terminal continuations remain native. A pending counter replacement choice defers lethal-damage state-based actions; saving and loading the pause neither repeats the rider nor commits the terminal twice.

Two prerequisites are deliberately bounded. Quoted static replacement admission compares both the exact Sokrates quotation and its entire native definition. Joint replay permits the existing full-prevention `consume_unpreventable=True` flag only when a rider is present. Neither change relaxes finite budget ownership or unsupported replacement operations.

The suite also covers zero, short-library, unpreventable, source departure, control change, old-object identity, expiry, native mill replacements, conditional hexproof, tap/sickness/defender costs and layer-6 removal/restoration.
