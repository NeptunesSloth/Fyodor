# Ashling's Prerogative

The existing odd/even entry choice and haste grant are joined by a bounded inverse chosen-mana-value selector for the native enters-tapped replacement. Invalid or missing choices match neither side. The replacement evaluates the prospective battlefield permanent, including current type-changing effects and permanent X=0, rather than the spell's announced X (CR 614.12, 202.3).

The inverse is bound to the other parity only after a valid Odd/Even choice exists. Existing positive parity and power selectors retain their meanings. Tests use actual enchantment and creature casts, Walking Ballista with X=1, Living Lands before a Forest enters, public choice save/replay and a fresh process followed by an opposing player's creature cast.
