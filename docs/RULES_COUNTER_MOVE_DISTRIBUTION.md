# Moving counters onto several creatures

Forgotten Ancient’s upkeep ability lets its controller allocate any number of its +1/+1
counters among other creatures, including opponents’ creatures. These are choices during
resolution, not targets (CR 608.2d, 122.5). The player chooses recipients and positive
amounts, then finishes, leaving any unallocated counters on Ancient. Choosing no moves
is allowed, as is declining the optional trigger.

The allocation is saved in the pending decision and replayed normally. No counters move
until allocation finishes. The source must still be the same object on the battlefield;
recipients prohibited from receiving that kind of counter are excluded. Source counters
are removed once, then all puts use the existing counter replacement machinery. Hardened
Scales and Doubling Season work, including a paused 616.1 ordering decision.
