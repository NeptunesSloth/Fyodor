# Quenchable Fire original payer

Canonical Oracle and the complete two official2009 rulings are preserved in canonical-rulings.json. One ruling states: “After Quenchable Fire resolves, the targeted player becomes able to perform a special action of paying {U}.” The other states that the special action affects the player originally targeted even if the initial damage is prevented or redirected. Those rulings predate the modern ability to target a planeswalker and do not explicitly discuss later planeswalker control changes.

The implementation reads the targeted planeswalker’s controller when Quenchable Fire resolves and creates that player’s payment permission. CR608.2h says required game/object information is determined only once when the effect is applied; CR116.2c identifies the later special action that can stop a delayed trigger. This is a player’s permission created by resolution, not an ability granted to and continually read from the planeswalker. It therefore stays with that original controller. The delayed damage separately retains the original object under CR603.7c; changing its controller does not change the damage recipient, while leaving/reentering makes a new object and prevents affecting the new incarnation. The rules are pinned to Comprehensive Rules2026-09-25 in /tmp/fyodor-cr20260925.txt.

The interpretation is tested natively: a p1-owned Ugin is controlled by p2 when targeted; after resolution it changes to p3’s control. While p3 has real priority with their Lightning Bolt on the stack, the payment action is unavailable to p3 and attempting it rejects without state mutation. Original controller p2 retains the private hint and pays at actual priority without changing the stack. The delayed trigger is removed and Ugin takes no further damage at the deadline. Separate existing cases verify actual Time Twist/new incarnation and initial Deflecting Palm prevention/reflection. The new disjoint case passed (quench-payer-control1.log); it is not claimed as an additional official ruling.
