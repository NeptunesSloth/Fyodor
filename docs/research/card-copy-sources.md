# Selecting original cards and casting their copies

The canonical family comprises Panoptic Mirror, Eye of the Storm, Zethi, Arcane Blademaster, Wildfire Devils, Spelltwine, Capricious Hellraiser, Wizard's Spellbook, Jacob Frye, Blue Mage's Cane, and Surge to Victory. The fixture retains actual Oracle metadata and faces; `backend/tests/test_rules_card_copy_sources.py` exercises their printed effects.

CR 707.12 creates genuine copies of cards, then casts those copies individually while the original effect resolves (608.2g). The originals remain in exile. The shared native copying and casting pipeline handles actual faces, targets, additional costs, casting events, ownership, and the disappearance of uncast copies. The new selection continuation retains exact object references. Its saved decision checks both the original selection pool and each original object ID, preventing a card that leaves and returns from becoming the old selected object.

Free casts, ordinary paid casts, and specified alternative costs are distinct. Blue Mage's Cane and the middle Spellbook row save their required alternative mana cost in the resolving effect's authority. The existing `Grant.alt_mana` path applies that alternative through ordinary casting, including cost increases, reductions and additional costs. It does not permit an ordinary or other printed alternative cost instead, and a mana-cost X is zero when this specified alternative contains no X (107.3b, 118.9). Old resolving effects with no such field retain their existing ordinary paid behavior.

Spellbook's printed sorcery restriction precedes its dice rows. The Oracle parser now joins a results table across that exact restriction, moving the restriction after the joined rows. Other unsupported timing text remains separate and manual. The effect uses the native recorded roll and native result conditions; actual tests exercise all three rows.

Eye of the Storm triggers on casting a card, excluding casting a copy of a card. The original trigger's caster receives the copied spells, even when another player controls the enchantment. Its accumulating linked pool uses the original source incarnation. Spelltwine merges its two actual exile results through standard reference fields so spell-line namespacing preserves their identity; an illegal target contributes no copy. Its mandatory cast choices use the native required-cast policy.

Surge to Victory creates a native repeated combat-damage watch for the current turn, binding the exact original exiled card when the watch is created. Panoptic's pending upkeep retains its source's old linked namespace after removal. Jacob's target union preserves the graveyard zone for both Assassin cards and cards with freerunning; a union of selectors must retain its candidate zone at the outer level.

Focused proofs include all ten actual effects, all three Spellbook rows, real partial/all-illegal Spelltwine targets, source removal with Panoptic's upkeep pending, free/ordinary/fixed costs, actual foreign ownership, X-zero modal card copies with the land face excluded, and no recursive Eye trigger for a cast copy. Fresh processes resume original-card selection, copy selection, a fixed alternative with Thalia/Baral cost changes, and a private Fling additional sacrifice cost. The latter preserves the fixed cost and deals actual damage using the sacrificed creature's power. The scenarios also validate JSON and history replay.

An additional native Jacob scenario exercises the non-Assassin freerunning branch with Eagle Vision: combat damage enables its actual printed alternative, the copied card is cast for {1}{U}, and it draws three cards. Spellbook tests also distinguish an Ancestral Vision copy with no mana cost: ordinary payment cannot cast it, while the specified {1} alternative can.
