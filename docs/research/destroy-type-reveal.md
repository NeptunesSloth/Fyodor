# Wild Magic Surge: destroying and revealing by shared card types

Oracle: “Destroy target permanent an opponent controls. Its controller reveals cards from the top of their library until they reveal a permanent card that shares a card type with that permanent. They put that card onto the battlefield and the rest on the bottom of their library in a random order.”

The controller and live card types are captured before destruction (CR 608.2h). The second instruction is unconditional: indestructibility, regeneration, or a destruction replacement do not prevent revealing. A destroyed token's types remain available after the token ceases to exist. Every card type counts, including Kindred; the revealed match must also be a permanent card, so a Kindred sorcery cannot qualify. A shared artifact or creature type suffices for an artifact creature. Type-changing effects apply to the destroyed permanent and to candidate library cards.

A typed leaf instruction snapshots these facts and queues native destruction followed by a typed reveal stage. The latter reveals only the matching prefix and delegates to native pick_looked, attachments.enter, and random rest_looked. This preserves entry choices, Aura attachments, entry replacement/prohibition behavior, and resumed resolution. If an entry is prohibited, the found card remains in the library while the other revealed cards are bottomed. If there is no match, the whole library is revealed and bottomed at random without drawing or shuffling. An illegal sole target stops before all these instructions.

Privacy regression: moving revealed cards within the same library does not itself clear revealed_to. Randomly bottomed cards must be hidden again, otherwise public library positions expose their random order. Test entry choices assert that the revealed prefix is public while the untouched suffix remains private, then assert that bottom positions are hidden after resolution. Every paused entry decision and final state round-trips save/load and replay.
