# Reveal consequences, current information and exact objects

The batch35 reveal family comprises Heirloom Blade, Descendants' Fury, Curse of Unbinding, Matter Reshaper, Caustic Bronco, Ruin Raider, Stronghold Arena, Sarkhan the Mad, Kaboom!, Amplifire, Paroxysm, Shapeshifter's Marrow, Hans Eriksson, and Guild Feud. Canonical data and official Wizards rulings are staged in `/tmp/fyodor-reveal-consequences35-{cards,rulings}.json`. Parent owns eleven cards; Counter implements Paroxysm/Marrow and their cold proofs, and Flash implements Hans through separate native leaves.

`reveal_consequences.py` snapshots revealed values before moving cards. It uses the existing reveal/look/cleanup pipeline and native optional decisions, entry, draw-independent hand placement, life loss, damage, base P/T, sacrifice and fight. Matter Reshaper follows only its original revealed library object into hand if an optional battlefield entry fails; redirected cards are not retrieved from a later zone. Caustic Bronco reads the original object's saddled fact even after death. Stronghold Arena's reveal decision precedes the reveal, and both independent kicker costs remain native. Sarkhan damages his own loyalty, preserves the sacrificed creature's controller for the Dragon, and uses native Dragon damage.

Heirloom reads dying creature LKI, including changed creature types. Fury offers only actual damage-event creatures that can be sacrificed, then reads the actual sacrifice receipt. No chosen/paid creature means no reveal. Unbinding captures the enchanted player's library but enters the found creature under the Curse controller. Amplifire sets base P/T from current revealed stats until its controller's next turn; no creature means0/0 and the subsequent SBA death. The spell/ability continues to finish its library placement first.

`reveal_target_sequences.py` processes Kaboom targets in APNAP player groups. Within a player's group, the caster orders player/planeswalker targets through a saved native order decision. Each reveal/damage/library placement finishes before the next target. The callback is registered before parser use and exercised in a genuine fresh-process save/resume. Zero targets resolves normally. Guild Feud gives the opponent their decision and entry first, then the controller their separate reveal/decision; exact actual creature arrivals fight in the same resolution before ETB triggers go on the stack.

Actual Heirloom gameplay exposed a shared type-line bug: `Time Lord Doctor` was parsed as three subtypes, while CR205.3m defines Time Lord as one type. A bounded lexer correction preserves that two-word subtype. The Tenth Doctor→The Master, Multiplied common-type test fails before the fix and passes afterward. Native changed-type LKI is separately verified with Wings of Velis Vel. Ordinary type/subtype/selectors and preceding reveal families are covered by the focused regression run.

Tests cover actual canonical effects, selected/declined/failed entries, optional and forbidden sacrifices, exhausted libraries, APNAP ordering, source controller and incarnation boundaries, current stats, both kicker costs, source death after saddle, normal history rebuild, JSON and cold choices. `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` records the exact audit status; parser admission alone is not a verified FULL promotion.
