# Narset Transcendent: granting rebound on the stack

The −2 creates a real, once-only delayed triggered ability for the next instant or sorcery cast from its controller's hand that turn. The casting event consumes the watcher; its trigger then resolves independently above the spell (CR 603.7). Removing Narset does not remove that watcher or trigger. Countering the delayed trigger consumes it without granting rebound, and countering the spell before that trigger resolves makes its exact-object reference do nothing.

The native next-cast grammar now admits its already typed cast-origin condition. A shared spell rider grants the actual Rebound keyword in layer 6 to the event's exact spell object. For a spell cast from hand, it also installs the native rebound resolution marker. Countering the spell removes that marker; a successful resolution uses the existing exile and optional next-upkeep cast machinery (CR 702.88a). Casting without paying from hand still qualifies. Casting from the graveyard, library, or exile does not consume Narset's watcher. A rebound cast from exile does not itself rebound again.

Canonical gameplay tests verify the full card, source removal, free hand casting, countering the original before/after the grant, Stifle countering the grant, independently continued hand-only watch after graveyard/library casting, unused end-of-turn expiry, accepted and declined upkeep offers, the granted keyword, and saved/replayed pending state. Existing creature spell riders, turn watches, and printed Ephemerate rebound tests guard the shared pipelines.

Rebound now records the exact object produced by exiling a resolving spell. Upkeep creation, resolution of the upkeep trigger, its saved optional cast offer, and a resumed cast all retain that identity. Leaving exile and returning never gives the new object the old permission. Older saves without the new map retain their previous compatibility behavior. Tests cover the card changing objects before upkeep, while the upkeep trigger is pending, and while its offer is saved/pending.

## Current-controller and physical-hand semantics

CR 109.5 makes “your” on the rebound static ability refer to the spell's current controller. PaymentRecord therefore stores the physical origin-zone player as an immutable casting fact. Resolution checks the live Rebound keyword and that player's identity against the current spell controller; it does not rely on a cast-time marker. Actual Commandeer can disable rebound by stealing an own-hand spell, restore it by returning control, or enable printed rebound on a spell originally cast from an opponent's hand when that opponent subsequently takes control. Pending rebound records the controller when its replacement applies (CR 603.7f), separately from card ownership, and offers the cast on that player's next upkeep.

The native “from your hand” event condition retains an explicit origin-owner constraint, so Narset's watcher is not consumed by Mindleech Mass casting a card from someone else's hand. Canonical Mindleech Mass, Staggershock, and Commandeer gameplay verifies both this exclusion and later control-enabled rebound. Sen Triplets remains outside this implementation because its own upkeep ability is not yet automated; no injected Sen Triplets permission is used. Old saves lacking the new origin and pending-controller facts use the prior caster/owner fallback.
