# Ability-sensitive power and toughness effects

CR 113.1, 113.10, 207.2a, 613.1f and 613.4: “creatures with no abilities” reads current abilities after layer 6 before applying a layer-7 modification. Reminder text and externally imposed restrictions are not abilities. Printed nonkeyword abilities remain abilities even when their conditions are not satisfied. Keyword counters grant abilities, with timestamps that interact with effects removing abilities.

Ruxa, Patient Professor and Muraganda Petroglyphs provide canonical fixed power/toughness cases. Jasmine Boreal of the Seven already uses the same selector for blocking restrictions and spell eligibility. A shared characteristic predicate must combine surviving keywords with nonkeyword ability lines. Unknown/unparsed printed lines still count. A removed sole printed keyword does not.

Pure layer-7 statics can select recipients after keyword, quoted, borrowed, and resolved granted abilities are incorporated. Sources already pass source-ability-loss filtering: Humility removes Ruxa’s anthem while Muraganda Petroglyphs boosts otherwise abilityless 1/1 creatures.

Ability-sensitive effects that also grant/remove abilities remain refused: their layer-6 dependencies and loops require separate support. Dynamic counted P/T effects remain outside this fixed-modifier/base-setting extension.

Gameplay cases cover both Oracle cards, controller scope, keyword and activated grants, removing a sole printed keyword, keyword-counter timestamps versus Turn to Frog, Humility, and tokens with no text or reminder text only. Completed gameplay cases verify event replay and save/load.
