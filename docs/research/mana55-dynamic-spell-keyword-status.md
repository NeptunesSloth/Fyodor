# Dynamic spell keyword costs (mana55)

Henzie, Toolbox Torre and Herigast, Erupting Nullkite now bind granted blitz and emerge costs to the actual spell being cast. Announced X remains separate from its mana payment, Henzie’s minimum mana value checks the announced spell X, and a frozen selected emerge payment survives sacrificing Herigast itself. Existing printed blitz costs also receive Henzie’s reduction, including the normal increase-before-reduction ordering with Sphere of Resistance. The reduction counts actual commander casts from the command zone.

All secondary abilities are automated: Henzie’s reduced blitz has haste, sacrifices at the next end step, and draws on death; Herigast’s cast trigger may exile the whole hand and draw three, including an empty hand or declining the option. Exiling the hand uses the native exile destination and creates no discard event.
