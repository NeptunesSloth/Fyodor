# Wave 110L refused cards

- The Eleventh Hour: chapter III "except it's a legendary Alien named Prisoner Zero" — whether "Alien" replaces or adds to the copied creature types (707.9b) is unsettled; refused.
- Battlefield Scrounger: the cost "Put three cards from your graveyard on the bottom of your library" lets the player order the cards (401.4) mid-cost-payment; the cost machinery has no ordering choice; refused.
- Lich: "If you would gain life, draw that many cards instead" would nest a draw (with its own replacement choices) inside the life-gain replacement loop; refused.
- Nephalia Academy: an optional ("you may reveal") discard replacement needs a decline branch in the 616 zone-replacement core, which has none; refused.
- Extraordinary Journey: "exile up to X target creatures" (variable target count from X) plus "entered from exile or was cast from exile" are both unread; refused.
- Prowl, Stoic Strategist // Prowl, Pursuit Vehicle: Convert and "plays a card exiled with Prowl" are both unread; refused.
- Reed Richards, Smartest Man: "the first time you would draw a card each turn except the first card you draw during each of your draw steps" — whether the draw step's own draw counts as "the first time" is unsettled (no ruling); refused.
- Parnesse, the Subtle Brush: "that spell" in "also copy that spell" is the copied spell, but the trigger event only carries the copy on the stack (targets may differ); unsettled; refused.
- Guile: "instead exile that spell and you may play that card" — when and for how long the card may be played is unsettled; refused.
- Lukka, Coppercoat Outcast: the granted "cast this card from exile as long as you control a Lukka planeswalker" needs a permission duration bound to a condition on any planeswalker (not the source); not modelled; refused.
- Urianger Augurelt: "Spells you cast this way cost {2} less" needs a cost reduction tied to one permission's use; a negative additional cost would apply before other increases (601.2f); refused.
- Curse of Inertia, Diaochan, Artful Beauty: "target … of their / an opponent's choice" on a triggered or non-delegated ability — who announces the target (115.1, 601.2c) is unsettled here; refused.
- Skyway Robber, Mairsil the Pretender, General Jarkeld, Throne of the Grim Captain, Kasmina, Enigma Sage, Jace's Machinations, Cryptic Spires (deck construction), Advancing the Spirit, Uldaros Theorix, Saruman of Many Colors, Bruna, Light of Alabaster, Indominus Rex, Alpha, Prowl: more than one unread mechanism each; not attempted.
- Primitive Justice, Avacyn's Judgment, Lightning Storm, Guardian Angel, Defensive Formation, Damping Engine, Lost in Thought, Fraying Line, Stolen Uniform, Melira, Long List of the Ents, Bamboozling Beeble: not attempted this wave (divided/variable targets, "any time you could cast an instant" payments, combat damage assignment by the defender, noted creature types, or ignore-this-effect payments need new core machinery).
