# Reveal-count activated mana abilities

Metalworker, Sacellum Godspeaker, and Rosheen, Roaring Prophet use complete canonical Oracle metadata in `backend/tests/fixtures/reveal_hand_mana_cards.json`. Metalworker produces two colorless mana per artifact card revealed; Godspeaker produces one green per creature card with power at least five; Rosheen produces two colorless per card with {X} in its mana cost, restricted to costs containing {X}. Rosheen's separate ETB mill-six/optional X-card recovery remains native and has an actual-cast gameplay test.

CR 605.1a/605.2: these are mana abilities even if the current hand contains no matching cards. They do not target, use loyalty, or move cards to or from a library. Classification recognizes the exact typed reveal-mana grammar before ordinary activated abilities. CR 605.3a/b/c: they resolve immediately without a stack object, can be activated during a mana payment, cannot be responded to, and cannot be activated again while their reveal choice is pending.

The ordinary control, activation prohibition, tap, and summoning-sickness checks remain native. The tap is paid before the hand reveal, which is an effect rather than an activation cost. The private choice permits zero, is asked even with no matching cards, and has no cancellation that could undo the paid tap. CR 701.20a/c: selected cards are shown publicly without changing zones. Native selectors evaluate card characteristics in the hand, including power and printed X symbols, rather than battlefield buffs or spell mana value. Multiple X symbols on one card still count as one revealed card.

The automatic mana solver does not assume elective hidden-card output or reveal cards without a player's choice. The normal self-payment interface lists these sources for explicit activation. Nested activation saves the original native payment decision, resolves the private reveal and production, and restores that payment with its locked context intact. JSON save/load stores source/ability snapshots, exact source identity, controller, replacement choices and the outer payment. Source-tagged ManaUnits preserve actual provenance and Rosheen's restriction.

CR 106.12b/616.1: replacements see the final production amount, after multiplying the per-card output by the actual reveal count. Multiple applicable native replacements have persisted order/type choices. Applicability is reevaluated after each replacement: Damping Sphere cannot be applied early to a one-mana production to evade it; Reflection can first make the production large enough for Sphere to apply. Replacement effects increase Rosheen's restricted production without removing the restriction (106.6/106.6a).

Native tapped-for-mana triggers add separate unrestricted bonus mana once per activation, with their own source identity. They are not multiplied per revealed card or included in the base production's multiplier. Attached-land bonuses also work when Ashaya makes the creature a land. CR 106.12a: zero revealed cards produce no mana-added/tapped-for-mana event and no bonus; the tap activation cost remains paid.

The legacy counter_unless and generic optional_payment affordability gates preserve a decision when an available manual reveal source exists. This permits the mana-ability window; it does not promise that the hand can pay. Actual payment still checks native affordability and pool coverage. Mana Leak tests cover payment, decline after production, a genuinely empty hand revealing zero, and rejected unaffordable Pay followed by decline. Rites of Refusal exercises the other payment branch with the opponent as payer. Neither branch deadlocks or pays an unaffordable cost.

Gameplay coverage includes zero/one/multiple reveals, empty private prompts, actual JSON-reloaded continuations and replay, normal and nested payments, no stack/opponent response, control/sickness/prohibitions, original source identity after departure, complete Rosheen casting/ETB, printed predicates and multiple X symbols, whole-production multipliers, persisted replacement ordering, amount-trigger applicability, and separate unrestricted bonuses from Forsaken Monument, Kinnan and Wild Growth. Related mana, replacement, provenance, optional-payment and nested-CPS regressions also run. Legacy tests now read the aggregate unrestricted pool through `tests.game_helpers.mana_pool`, including remembered source-tagged units.
