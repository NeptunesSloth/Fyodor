# Counter distribution during resolution

Vastwood Hydra, Feast of the Victorious Dead, Invoke Justice, Stumpsquall Hydra, and Blessing of Frost use untargeted recipients. Their canonical Oracle text is preserved in `tests/fixtures/counter_distribution_cards.json`.

CR 608.2d determines these recipients and allocations during resolution. This differs from native target division at announcement (601.2d). The new queue captures an exact-object recipient pool and freezes the amount at resolution, chooses recipients, then assigns the entire total among a nonempty selection with at least one per chosen recipient. Choosing zero recipients is permitted by “any number” (107.1c); a nonempty selection cannot discard an arbitrary remainder. Impossible counter recipients are excluded; hexproof/protection do not bar an untargeted recipient. Ordinary replacement effects still determine the resulting placements.

All shares are placed in one native counter batch after all choices complete. Replacement-order pauses save the original shares and rerun only the native placement plan; they never reallocate or double counters twice. Typed amounts avoid enumerating a separate option for every possible quantity. Exact object references prevent a returned physical card from inheriting an older object's allocation. Save/load and fresh-process tests cover multiple recipient and amount decisions.

Vastwood uses source LKI counters. Feast freezes one shared resolution-time death count before native life gain and distribution. Invoke's permanent and player remain announced targets, while its later recipients are chosen after the permanent returns (including that new permanent if eligible). Stumpsquall's union includes itself and commanders of every controller. Blessing reads actual Snow-source mana provenance from the existing payment record, then evaluates its native power-four draw count after placement; a spell copy receives no Snow allocation.

A required canonical Vastwood edge exposed an existing CR 704.7 bug: lethal -1/-1 counters and simultaneous counter annihilation were processed before the departing object's snapshot. The bounded SBA correction freezes full snapshots/derived traits only for objects both dying and annihilating counters in the same check, then restores that exact departure LKI and history types before trigger dispatch. Native counter-removal actions/events are preserved. Actual Black Sun's Zenith tests prove Vastwood distributes its pre-cancellation counters, Protean Hydra still produces every removed-counter trigger, and Modular transfers the pre-SBA count.

Rules source: pinned Comprehensive Rules 2026-09-25, 107.1c, 601.2d, 608.2d, 704.7. Canonical rulings: Scryfall/Gatherer Vastwood Hydra (non-target allocation and pre-SBA counters), Invoke Justice (recipients chosen after return), Blessing of Frost (post-placement draw count, actual Snow mana, copies spend zero).
