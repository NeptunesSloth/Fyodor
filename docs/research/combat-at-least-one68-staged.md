# One-attacker requirements — unhooked design

Next exact candidates: Seeker of Slaanesh and Trove of Temptation. Both are PARTIAL in checkpoint50, with only their declaration-wide requirement unsupported. Their complete canonicals and all official rulings are in `/tmp/combat67-requirements-canonical-rulings.json`.

The unregistered leaf `backend/app/rules/combat_at_least_one68.py` emits source/controller-relative group markers. The draft `/tmp/combat68-at-least-one-kernel-draft.patch` extends native requirement origin metadata and adds a fulfilled-group bitmask to the existing legal declaration optimizer. A group contributes its independent weight once if any declared creature attacks a qualifying destination. Groups with identical destination sets are compressed while preserving summed source/ability multiplicity. Seeker permits any attack destination; Trove counts only its source controller and that player's planeswalkers, excluding battles. Existing attack caps, restrictions, unpaid attack costs, per-creature requirements and conditional67 requirements remain in the same maximization.

No shared hooks, isolated runtime tests or admissions yet. Draft built against the isolated conditional67 kernel, so requires its closure first. Required next proofs: native haste/attack and Trove Treasure trigger, one versus many attackers, either of two available attackers, zero eligible, costs/restrictions/caps, overlapping competing sources, goad/conditional interaction, source control changes, planeswalker versus Battle destination, entering-attacking bypass and genuine cold save/reload.
