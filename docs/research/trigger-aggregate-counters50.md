# Littjara Kinseekers: a common creature type and an intervening condition

The exact trigger delegates counter placement and scry to native grammar. Its intervening condition uses the existing shared_type maximum over creatures its ability's controller currently controls, checked at entry and through ordinary only_if before both resolving effects. A pairwise chain of creature types does not establish one type common to all three. Native changeling characteristics supply every creature type. The three current creatures need not be the same three as at entry.

Ten focused native cases pass, including two fresh-process reloads. They cover actual paid casting, native Changeling plus Bears establishing a common type, actual 3/5 counter stats and scry, the pairwise-only negative, native Turn to Frog breaking the group before resolution and gating both effects, Doubling Season and Solemnity without incorrectly gating scry, a replacement trio, no retroactive trigger, and the original source UID after a physical successor returns. All seven official rulings were read. Sixty-four related value/condition checks, scoped mypy and owned Ruff pass. Review manifest: /tmp/counter50-kin-review-manifest.json. Exact four-line hook: /tmp/counter50-kin-hooks-proposal.patch.

Rumbling Ruin is explicitly excluded. Its prototype/tests are preserved only under /tmp/counter50-rumbling-experiment.
