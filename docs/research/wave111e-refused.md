# Wave 111E refusals (Star Trek, group E; CR 2026-09-25, Oracle text of 2026-10-30, no official rulings published yet)

- **All Good Things...** — "IV — If all cards exiled with this Saga share a card type, you gain 10 life.": the CR doesn't define "all … share a card type" for zero or one card (a Saga entering with lore counters, cards that left exile, an empty hand), and no ruling settles it yet.
- **Chief Montgomery Scott** — "It enters with X additional +1/+1 counters on it if it's a creature and X additional charge counters on it if it's a Spacecraft": whether a Spacecraft card whose X charge counters reach its station threshold "is a creature" as it enters (and so also gets the +1/+1 counters) is unsettled by 614.12 (both parts are one replacement effect); no ruling yet.
