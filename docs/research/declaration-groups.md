# Whole-declaration companion restrictions

Okk, Orcish Conscripts, Scarred Puma, and Ekundu Cyclops require the complete declared attacker or blocker group. These checks share the existing “can't attack/block alone” foundation. They inspect completed characteristics: current power, colors, granted/removed abilities, and unique declared creatures. A blocker assigned to two attackers remains one creature and cannot provide its own companion. Different attackers/defenders can share a declaration group.

The native statics parser records the closed greater-power, minimum-three, colored-companion, and conditional-Ekundu rules. Actual declarations first satisfy individual legality and then the group predicate, before costs or any declaration actions commit. Ekundu's conditional requirement is active when another controlled creature is actually declared attacking; it cannot force an otherwise empty attack.

Attack requirement optimization includes optional supporting creatures, not only creatures with requirements. A memoized profile records group size up to three, greatest selected power, greatest selected power requiring a stronger companion, minimum group size, and black/green companion counts up to two. Native defender/global attack capacities and goad/player-destination scores are retained. Distinct destinations are evaluated separately.

The blocking optimizer considers native legal target subsets up to each blocker's block limit. Its memoized state tracks truncated actual/effective per-attacker counts, group profile, remaining blocker capacity, and satisfied typed-blocker requirements. It checks menace, maximum-one, all-creatures blocking restrictions, player/global caps, individual pair legality, and native band expansion. It scores must-block, named-object/Lure, must-be-blocked, two-blocker and typed-blocker requirements over complete legal declarations. Optional support can block a different attacker. Optimizers skip work when no relevant requirements exist; no arbitrary search cutoff accepts an unproved declaration.

New hypothetical payments are never compelled. A voluntarily included paid attacker at its already chosen destination, or an already selected paid blocker, can supply support without another fee. Other fee-bearing companions are excluded from the hypothetical completion. Existing native payment, locking, and continuation paths remain unchanged.

Official CR508.1c–d and509.1b–c require maximizing obeyed requirements without violating restrictions and exempt additional costs. CR509.1c's own example requires an otherwise optional creature to join a required blocker against menace. CR506.5 counts declared creatures rather than assignments. In multiplayer, CR802.4b explicitly ignores other defending players' attackers/blockers when judging one player's declaration; no cross-player speculative support is counted. Text checked against Wizards' official April4,2025 comprehensive rules (download retained at /tmp/fyodor-cr27.txt); the shared engine's normal current rules remain authoritative.

Canonical tests cover all four cards, existing Mogg Flunkies, actual Bloodthirsty Blade goad, Fumiko, Lure, Silent Arbiter, Goblin War Drums, High Ground, Brainwash/Oppressive Rays, current Giant Growth power and Lifelace colors, Dress Down, replay, and fresh-process attacker/blocker declaration restoration.

The shared attacker payment tail now uses a typed prepared terminal like blocking. Attacker and card-defender object identities and the original tap/vigilance choice are captured before payment. Manual payment stores the terminal in `paid_then`; completion does not rerun already-checked declaration legality or choose a new cost. A departed/reentered attacker is filtered by its exact original object; remaining legal-declaration members continue attacking even if their former companion has departed. Bands are trimmed to surviving original objects. Old saved terminals without identity maps retain their legacy fallback.

Only prepared attacker/blocker terminals bypass the legacy nested-payment object reservation. Other costs and spell/ability reservations are unchanged. A canonical Phyrexian Altar example sacrifices declared Craw Wurm while paying Brainwash's attack fee for Okk; Okk alone survives into combat and the Wurm remains in its graveyard. The same exact-once payment and commit survive a fresh process. Recorded zone-move race tests separately ensure the same physical card returning as a new object is not incorrectly reused.
