# Effects that read direct sacrifice costs

Fifteen canonical cards use shared direct-cost last-known information: Pyre of Heroes, Soldevi Adnate, Anchor to Reality, Baba Lysaga, Night Witch, Desperate Plea, Adipose Offspring, Soulblast, Tormented Thoughts, Sanguine Praetor, Mind Extraction, Endemic Plague, Eye of Yawgmoth, Freyalise Supplicant, Fiery Bombardment, and Agency Coroner.

The new effect leaf binds the original sacrificed object IDs from the resolving spell or activated ability's payment receipt through the existing `this_way.cost_objects` and standard `ref:` machinery. It reads their pre-sacrifice characteristics, then inserts native damage, discard, look/pick/exile, destruction, search, return, scry, or token instructions into the saved continuation. Costs remain paid exactly once. Mana-source sacrifices are excluded from direct costs. Copies retain the original paid-object decisions and last-known information.

Power sums include negative power before the final damage clamp. Freyalise rounds half power down. Fiery Bombardment counts each red mana symbol once, independently of how its mana cost was paid. Mind Extraction matches any paid color; colorless matches none. Endemic Plague matches any shared creature subtype and prevents regeneration. Pyre combines the shared subtype requirement with exact sacrificed mana value plus one. Anchor reads the found permanent's current post-entry characteristics: an Imposter Mech copying a larger creature uses its copied mana value rather than its original library mana value. Desperate Plea's power comparison is an effect condition, preserving its ordinary announced target and independent other mode.

Two small native cost extensions preserve mandatory payment semantics. `Sacrifice up to N` reuses the existing optional bounded group flag. `Sacrifice all` records a mandatory full group, allows an empty group, rejects partial groups, and rejects payment when a required permanent cannot be sacrificed. Soulblast therefore cannot pay by sacrificing only the legal subset.

Agency Coroner captures suspected status in the pre-move snapshot. The optional field distinguishes old saves that lack this information and offers an explicit manual continuation instead of assuming a false result. Adipose reads the original cast receipt already transferred to the entered permanent, including copied emerge decisions and sacrificed toughness; it never reads the trigger's own payment.

Soldevi needs no new payment architecture. Existing native `sacrificed_mv` mana production reads the selected creature before paying and produces mana immediately. Its bounded cost-selector union duplicates Creature, controller, zone, and other-object constraints inside both the colored-creature and artifact-creature branches. Thus noncreature artifacts and opponents' creatures cannot pay the cost.

Validation: 45 distinct canonical cases and 76 existing activation-cost/any-number regressions passed. Canonical coverage includes every positive effect, both Adipose and Agency outcomes, mandatory-all empty/subset/prohibition cases, negative-power sums, physical-card movement back to a hidden library, prospective copy mana value, native spell copying, replay/save-load, and independent-process resumes for Eye and Soldevi. All fifteen canonical coverage assertions pass. Parent catalog/type/core checks remain the authority for batch promotion totals.
