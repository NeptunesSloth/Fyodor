# Jinxed Choker: the receiving opponent puts the counter

The exact end-step trigger delegates to the ordinary native target-opponent control transfer, then places its charge counter through native add_counters with the selected target as its actual putter. Counter replacements therefore inspect the receiving player's identity. The existing upkeep trigger freezes its controller when it triggers and determines the original source's counter count as it resolves. The already approved fixed counter-alternative primitive handles the printed paid activation.

The staged runtime passes fourteen whole-card cases, including two genuine fresh-process reloads, and 68 related controller/counter replacement cases. Witnesses include paid source casting, transfer to either opponent, damage to the new controller on their upkeep, the receiving player's Vorinclex doubling the charge counter, Solemnity allowing the control transfer while preventing the counter, new-controller activation and former-controller rejection, a blinked successor excluded from the old trigger, transfer already accomplished before resolution, removing counters before upkeep damage, native Memnarch theft while the old upkeep trigger retains its original player, and departed-source last-known counters. Both official rulings were read. Scoped types and Ruff pass.
