# Tamiyo, Field Researcher: watching selected creatures

The +1 chooses zero, one, or two target creatures. Resolving the loyalty ability creates a native delayed watcher bound to the legal targets' exact object IDs, with its original controller and an expiration at the start of that player's next turn (CR 603.7, 603.7c–e, 611.2a). The chosen creatures need not belong to that player. Changing their controller or losing Tamiyo does not remove the watcher; a creature that leaves and returns is a new object and is no longer watched (CR 400.7).

A bounded target-group leaf parser supplies the native target group, delayed effects, and next-turn duration. This wording explicitly requests source-batched damage matching. The shared watch dispatcher joins simultaneous damage to several recipients by damage batch and source object ID, just as ordinary “deals damage” triggers do. Thus a trampling creature hitting two blockers and a player draws once; two selected creatures each dealing damage draw twice; first-strike and regular damage are separate events. Generic unannotated damage watches with unsupported reference shapes remain refused.

Canonical Tamiyo metadata is shared with the existing emblem tests. New gameplay coverage checks the entire card's FULL classification, zero/one/two targets, multiple blockers plus trample and double strike, Tamiyo leaving, a watched creature changing controller and damaging its new controller's opponent, expiration on the original controller's next turn, exact object identity after blinking, and replay/save verification. Existing turn-watch and next-turn suites guard the reused delayed-trigger machinery.
