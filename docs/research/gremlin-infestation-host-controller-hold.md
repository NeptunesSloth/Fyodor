# Gremlin Infestation: held for a host-controller information provider

No live hook, promotion, or shared kernel edit. A TMP-only experiment replaces only the exact missing damage instruction's `enchanted artifact's controller` with the existing native `enchanted creature's controller` parser alias. The resulting effect uses `host_controller`; its native `_cards(host)` helper requires the host to remain on the battlefield.

A real actual-database native paid Gremlin Infestation cast on player 2's Icy Manipulator reproduces the boundary. With its end-step trigger pending, a paid Boomerang returning the Aura while the artifact survives correctly deals 2 damage to player 2. A paid Naturalize destroying the artifact, followed by the Aura's native state-based death, incorrectly deals zero damage instead of 2. This rules out admitting the parser alias alone. The missing information provider must recover the enchanted object's last-known controller without confusing a successor incarnation.

Reproducer: `/tmp/counter65-host-controller-native-probe.py`; isolated app `/tmp/counter65-probe-stage`; final native failure `/tmp/counter65-host-controller-native-probe.log`. No new repository rules module or registration was installed.

Full actual canonical and the single official ruling were read: `/tmp/counter65-gremlin-canonical.json` and `/tmp/counter65-gremlin-rulings.json`. The ruling confirms the Aura controller creates the Gremlin when its enchanted artifact dies. Exact failure manifest and artifact hashes: `/tmp/counter65-gremlin-hold-manifest.json`.
