# Costs attached to individual creatures attacking or blocking

Qal Sisma Behemoth, Oppressive Rays, Cowed by Wisdom, Myr Prototype, and Whipgrass Entangler share a per-creature mana cost to attack or block. The cost applies regardless of the defending player or permanent. It differs from Propaganda's existing per-defender attack tax.

The new `creature_combat_cost` static stores a self/attached subject, a real mana cost, and an optional native count expression. Declaration evaluation reads completed characteristics: the creature's own current definitions and current definitions of its actually attached Aura sources. Thus a resolved Whipgrass grant remains a real layer6 ability, later ability loss removes it, and a grant applied after an earlier ability-loss effect can apply. Multiple identical grants have different native ability indices and each counts. No permanently imposed workaround rule survives removal incorrectly.

Native values read the Aura controller's hand size, the creature's +1/+1 counters, or all Clerics on the battlefield. Aura control changes update whose hand is counted. The total joins the existing native attack/block payment continuations; no new payment engine is introduced. Attack payment also honors a preexisting locked cost when mana production has reduced the live count to zero. An actual Phyrexian Altar sacrifices the last Cleric during a pending Whipgrass attack payment, proving the original cost is still paid once.

Requirements cannot force additional payments (CR508.1d,509.1c): Fumiko and Lure cases allow declining the paid attack/block even with enough mana available. A creature declared blocking multiple attackers pays its per-creature cost once. Costs are determined before mana abilities and are paid transactionally (CR508.1h–j,509.1d–f).

Oppressive Rays separately modifies activated ability mana costs for its current attached host. Its exact attached-host predicate applies to any controller and excludes unrelated sources. Ordinary Azure Mage and actual Llanowar Elves cover nonmana and mana activations respectively; source removal must remove the modifier. The native mana-activation integration uses the same effective, locked input cost for explicit activation, nested activation, and automatic mana planning. Actual Llanowar Elves tests verify insufficient payment leaves the source untapped, payment consumes the tax exactly once, unrelated Elves remain untaxed, and actual Disenchant restores the former host’s untaxed activation. Separate integration cases cover source departure during payment and reductions to the taxed cost.

Canonical tests additionally cover underpayment rollback, both attacker and blocker declarations, zero/dynamic costs, Myr's real upkeep counter, source departure and expiry, actual Disenchant, actual Dress Down order against Whipgrass grants, saved games, deterministic replay, and fresh-process restoration of attack and block mana-payment decisions. Payment tests use legally printed mana sources; no direct-library-movement ability is treated as a mana ability.
