# Contextual mana outcomes

The fifteen-card family uses native effect contexts, mana units, payment continuations, delayed triggers and saved untap operations. The allocator is unchanged. The canonical fixture and actual gameplay suite are `backend/tests/fixtures/contextual_mana_outcomes_cards.json` and `backend/tests/test_rules_contextual_mana_outcomes.py`.

The cards are Altar of the Pantheon, Trenzalore Clocktower, Tarnation Vista, Titans' Nest, Niko Defies Destiny, Smoky Lounge // Misty Salon, Mana Short, Power Sink, Energy Tap, Plasm Capture, Conduit of Storms // Conduit of Emrakul, Open the Omenpaths, Blood on the Snow, Undiscovered Paradise and Upwelling.

Mana abilities use real nonstack bodies; ordinary triggers and targeted abilities retain the stack. Trenzalore's counter addition is immediate, while its twelve-counter removal and exile are actual activation costs before its ordinary shuffle/draw ability. Tarnation computes represented colors among current monocolored permanents after its input cost is paid, using the explicit native activation path. It produces each represented color once, passes output through native mana replacements, and can legally produce zero. Explicit zero-output count abilities remain activatable, pay their costs and tap; AUTO payment omits zero-output sources. No mana-production trigger is manufactured for zero output.

Altar's devotion adjustment is counted once per permanent for either one color or a combination. CR 700.5a computes devotion after copy, control and text-changing effects, before other characteristic modifications. The official Altar ruling explicitly preserves the bonus through ability loss. Both ordinary devotion values and Nykthos's real mana production use the same calculation, avoiding a full-characteristics recursion through Gods.

Restrictions stay with actual mana units. Titans' Nest checks the effective spell's mana cost for X, independently of chosen X or additional costs. Niko's mana may pay the foretell special action or a spell with foretell, from any origin. Its first chapter counts owned foretold objects rather than every card having foretell; its third chapter uses a native graveyard selector. Smoky Lounge's mana pays Room spells or unlocking; Misty Salon counts unlocked individual doors under the resolving controller's control. Open the Omenpaths requires two distinct colors and restricts all four resulting units together.

Mana Short and Power Sink explicitly empty selected players' pools. Upwelling prevents boundary emptying; it does not prevent an explicit loss instruction. Kruphix's conversion applies to both. Explicit emptying uses the native single-player `clear_mana` action and clamps retained-mana records. Colorless conversion chunks only oversized plain-pool deltas into existing valid action sizes; source units are replaced once and retain source identity, restrictions and riders. No UI limits were widened. A real Forest-produced unit plus 1100 plain mana is covered for both emptying and conversion, alongside Worldpurge and existing conversion regressions.

Power Sink's unpaid consequences depend on the payment outcome, including when the spell cannot be countered; they use its original controller and current lands with a mana ability. Energy Tap uses native target legality and effective permanent mana value. Plasm Capture captures stack mana value before countering and waits for the next first main phase. Each Conduit face creates a next-main-phase delayed trigger limited to the current turn. Blood on the Snow selects its return after destruction, uses actually spent snow-source tags and never announces that return as a target; spell copies have no spent snow mana.

Undiscovered Paradise stores a typed original card/object reference and the ORIGINAL ACTIVATOR. Its operation happens during that player's next actual untap step, including an extra untap this turn. A skipped untap does not consume it, changing control does not change its scheduled player, and a departed original object is not followed to a new incarnation. The official ruling says this is not replacing the land's untapping and still returns a land prevented from untapping. The native normal-turn and extra-untap paths share the operation hook, without making an upkeep trigger or giving an untap priority window.

Canonical positive tests cover every card, both Saga/Room/transform faces where applicable, and all three Niko chapters. Fresh-process save/replay tests cover Paradise through a skipped untap, Omenpaths's second color, Plasm's delayed amount, Blood's graveyard selection and Titans' selected exile cost. Further cases cover original source identity, controller transfer, actual Shadow of the Second Sun extra beginning phases, large pools, real Nykthos, ability loss, spending restrictions and native spell modes.

Research used the pinned comprehensive rules dated 2026-09-25 and current canonical Oracle plus official WotC rulings retrieved through Scryfall. Preparation artifacts are `/tmp/fyodor-mana-outcomes38-plan.md`, the corresponding canonical JSON and official-rulings JSON. Nexos, Vazi, Boreal Outrider, Sunken Palace and Domri remain staged separately: their missing receipt/slot/symbolic-cost prerequisites are not claimed by this family.
