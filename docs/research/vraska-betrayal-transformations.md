# Vraska, Betrayal's Sting

Oracle source: the repository's imported Scryfall Oracle database, checked 2026-10-19. Rules: CR 205.1a (type/subtype replacement), 205.4b (supertypes), 611.2a/c (permanent duration and affected objects), 613.1d/f and 613.7 (type/ability layers and timestamps), 608.2h (information on resolution), 122.6/616.1 (counter additions and replacements), and 704.5 (state-based actions).

The -2 ability sets Artifact and replaces artifact subtypes with Treasure. Creature and other removed card types' subtypes cease to apply. An artifact Equipment creature consequently loses Equipment as well as its creature types. Name, colors, mana cost/value, supertype, controller and counters remain unchanged. The effect removes old abilities and grants the checked quoted Treasure mana ability in the same layer-6 effect. The grant survives its own wipe; earlier grants are removed and later grants apply normally. Later ability removal can suppress the Treasure mana ability. The continuous effect survives Vraska leaving and ends applying when the target changes objects. Normal attachment state-based actions handle Equipment and creature-only Auras.

The -9 ability targets any player. It computes max(0, 9 - current poison) on resolution and puts that many counters through the ordinary counter-addition pipeline, by Vraska's ability controller. It does not set the player's total to nine. Vorinclex therefore doubles or halves the amount according to the player putting the counters, while Winding Constrictor modifies counters its controller receives. The affected player orders applicable replacements, with paused choices retained through save/load. At nine or more poison no counter event is created. Prevention such as Melira and Solemnity applies normally.

Both grammars are reusable typed descriptions, without card-name checks. Unsupported artifact subtypes, unrecognized granted abilities and unrecognized counter-count words fail closed. Existing older saves default the optional artifact-subtype-setting field to None.

Actual Oracle fixture tests cover loyalty costs/timing, Compleated payments, the zero-loyalty draw/life/proliferate ability, permanent Treasure conversion including equipment subtypes and exact retained traits, functioning granted mana, early/later ability grants and wipes, source departure/blink, poison below/equal/above nine for self and opponents, live counts, doubling, halving, prevention and ordered replacement choices. Gameplay is replayed and saved/loaded at ordinary and paused points.
