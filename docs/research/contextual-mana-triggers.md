# Contextual mana events and producers

Researched against pinned CR 2026-09-25: 106.3, 106.6, 106.12a, 107.3, 400.7a, 603.4, 605.1–605.4 and 707.10. Canonical cards and official rulings were fetched for the batch; fixtures preserve actual mana values, colors, types and ability text.

The twelve modeled cards are Keeper of Progenitus, Overabundance, Barbflare Gremlin, Snowfall, Adarkar Unicorn, Unbound Flourishing, Vazi, Keen Negotiator, Boreal Outrider, Sunken Palace, Domri, Chaos Bringer, Mana Cache and Rhystic Cave. Winter's Night remains outside this admission because its World supertype requires a separate World-rule proof. Jeweled Amulet and Ice Cauldron remain outside this admission until linked note, copied payment and declined-exile semantics are settled.

Additional mana is produced by the triggered ability's source, separately from the tapped permanent. Restricted or optional profiles use explicit activation and native saved choices; they do not promise unpredictable aggregate output to automatic payment. Base instructions and riders finish before the frozen triggered mana instances resolve. Their order follows native seat order, and each producer retains its exact object identity, output choice and restriction. Overabundance's damage comes from the enchantment; Barbflare's damage comes from the tapped land. Snowfall's extra blue is independent of the Island's output and is restricted specifically to cumulative-upkeep payment. Its snow condition is checked when the triggered mana ability resolves, using the original land or its last known information.

Current CR106.12a requires actual mana production to trigger a tapped-for-mana ability. An old Overabundance ruling says zero output still triggers its damage, but conflicts with current CR and Rhystic Cave's official ruling. The implementation follows the pinned current CR: no output creates no tapped-for-mana trigger.

Actual ability activations now freeze actor, source incarnation, original X-symbol cost and direct input-payment facts, including mana-source traits. Mana abilities create no artificial stack objects. A filter's Treasure input can trigger Vazi for that filter activation; it does not mark the filter's output as Treasure mana when that output pays a later spell. Sunken Palace attaches its copy trigger to the actual unit spent; ordinary stack abilities and spells use native event/LKI copies. Spending that unit on a true mana ability never fabricates a stack copy.

Boreal Outrider uses a new optional receipt mapping of actual snow-source mana by color/type. Separate aggregate mana colors and source tags cannot establish that the same unit had both properties. New payments capture the mapping, copies record zero actual spending, and older records retain an unknown value. Boreal's resolution checks the exact creature spell and its current colors before installing its native entry counter.

Unbound Flourishing changes current spell X through replayable stack metadata, without changing the amount paid or replaying a payment. Native copies retain X, modes, targets and additional-cost decisions while spending no new mana. Additional X costs do not make a spell's mana cost contain X, and a nonmana cost merely described with X is distinct from the mana symbol.

Domri's loyalty ability uses the stack. Its mana carries a creature-spell riot rider, which grants a real layer-6 ability to that spell and follows the resulting permanent through native CR400.7a tracking. Native riot choices now handle multiple instances independently. A counter prohibition forces haste for riot rather than offering an impossible counter choice.

Mana Cache separates the activating player from the enchantment's controller: source counters pay once, and the activating player gets the mana. Its timing restriction is checked against that player. Rhystic Cave requires priority, chooses a color before optional payments, offers payment in active-player turn order, and completes through the existing immediate mana continuation. It is excluded from automatic payment and cannot be activated inside another casting/activation payment.

Cast triggers that refer to the cast spell's X retain an explicit exact-object reference and read its current X as they resolve; after the spell is countered they read stack LKI. This preserves Unbound's official choice of resolving its doubling before or after Hydroid Krasis/Zaxara's trigger. An independent optional `resolved_x` records a permanent spell's actual X from the native stack-to-battlefield snapshot, for effects such as Gadwick's ETB. The originally paid/announced `PaymentRecord.x` is unchanged; old saves fall back to it when the newer snapshot field is absent. Copied permanent spells get their own actual resolved X and continue to spend no mana.
