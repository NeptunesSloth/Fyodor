# Nonhand cast quantities and permanent spell-copy riders

Surge of Brilliance and Impending Flux read actual cast history at resolution.
The controller's spells cast from any known zone other than hand count; copied
spells do not. A nonhand cast of the resolving spell includes itself. Unknown
legacy origins are not silently classified as nonhand.

The Twelfth Doctor uses the native first-nonhand-cast ledger and demonstrate
trigger pipeline. Earlier nonhand casts consume the first cast even if the
Doctor entered later. Hand casts do not. Intrinsic demonstrate and independent
Doctor grants generate their own instances. Spell copies use native stack LKI,
new-target choices and copy events; only the Doctor controller's copy adds its
counter.

Spider-Verse and Mizzix, Replica Rider compile to native optional spell copy,
paid conditional continuation and permanent-only copy gains. Mizzix uses the
existing hybrid payment and delayed sacrifice machinery. Spider's native action
binding becomes successful only when a copy is made, so declining or attempting
to copy Display of Power does not consume the once-per-turn action. Copies are
not actual casts. Permanent spell copies become tokens under CR111.12 and keep
haste on the resulting object; Mizzix's granted sacrifice ability follows it.

Canonical gameplay tests cover draw/damage history, the first-cast boundary,
multiple demonstrate instances, optional decline, uncopyable spells, permanent
copy haste and end-step sacrifice, replay and fresh-process payment resume.
Existing demonstrate/cascade/copy suites provide adjacent regression coverage.
