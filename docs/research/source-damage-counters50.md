# Talon of Pain and Night Dealings: actual source control and damage recipients

The exact printed triggers delegate their counter instructions to existing native grammar. Talon uses the native deal-damage event with an explicit controller-filtered, source-excluding selector spanning battlefield, stack and card zones; this retains instant, sorcery, permanent and last-known sources. Its native recipient predicate requires a player other than its controller and positive damage. Night Dealings delegates to the existing native source-controller event and captured damage amount; the recipient predicate explicitly tests for a different player. No shared algorithm, primitive or state field is added.

The native proof suite covers paid source casting, actual Lightning Bolt after its source has left the stack, simultaneous Flame Rift producing separate opponent triggers, self/creature recipients and opponent sources excluded, paid Talon counter removal and damage without self-recursion, paid Night Dealings removal/search/reveal for an exact nonland mana value, and insufficient counter costs rejected atomically. Further witnesses cover creature combat, Fog preventing all combat damage, Doubling Season and Solemnity, actual Ghostly Flicker or Flicker of Fate preserving original object identities, source control changing after a trigger is queued, and a real paid spell changing controller away from its original owner/caster before dealing damage. Four fresh-process cases preserve original source events, Talon's paid activation, and Night Dealings' already paid search decision. Neither card has official rulings in the current Scryfall ruling feed; both complete canonical records and empty feeds were read.
