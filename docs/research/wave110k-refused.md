# Wave 110K — refused or not attempted (cloud list K)

One line each. "Refused" = a rules question is unsettled or the foundation would be a guess; "not attempted" = needs a foundation too big for this wave.

- Thief of Blood: refused — "as it enters, remove all counters from all permanents" must change other permanents inside the entry-counter computation, which the engine also runs as a look-ahead.
- Scrap Welder, Fabrication Foundry: refused — X comes from a cost chosen after targets (601.2c vs 601.2h), but it limits which card may be targeted.
- Arcum's Sleigh: refused — "defending player" outside an attack trigger is undefined in multiplayer (506.2); no ruling found.
- Contractual Safeguard: built in wave 110J (last cards): the player picks a creature and a kind of counter on it, each other creature gets one; the addendum's shield counter may be chosen (ruling 2022-04-29).
- The Great Aurora: refused — whether tokens count toward "that many" (they cease to exist in the library) is unsettled.
- Grime Gorger: refused — "up to one card of each card type": a multi-type card filling two types is unsettled.
- Alpine Moon, Threefold Signal, Turtles Forever: refused — chosen-name land rewriting, granted replicate, a search outside the game (see waves 100 and 110F).
- Homura, Human Ascendant // Homura's Essence: not attempted — "return it to the battlefield flipped" has no move parameter for the flipped status (710).
- Aegis of the Legion: not attempted — the "mentors" event (702.134c) is not emitted when the mentor trigger resolves.
- Arni Metalbrow: not attempted — no "enters attacking" trigger event; the five places that put a creature onto the battlefield attacking would each have to emit one.
- Mana Web, Benthic Explorers: not attempted — no "land is tapped for mana" trigger event or "could produce any type that land could" evaluation.
- Riku of Many Paths: not attempted — "choose up to X modes, X the number of modes chosen for that spell" needs a spell-mode record and a variable modal trigger.
- Inniaz, the Gale Force: not attempted — a ring of control changes among all players, each to a permanent of the controller's choice.
- Idris, Soul of the TARDIS, Clandestine Chameleon, The Book of Vile Darkness: not attempted — abilities borrowed from linked exiled cards or stickers.
- Space Beleren, Ichor Elixir: not attempted — sectors (Space sculptor) and planar dice.
- Silhouette: not attempted — prevention only of damage caused by a spell or ability that targets the creature needs the resolving stack object during damage.
- Fight the _____ Fight: not attempted — "name sticker with eight or more letters" (letters of a multi-word sticker) is unsettled; plus a reflexive fight.
- Illusionary Mask, Emblazoned Golem: not attempted — face-down casts from X mana; an X payment with at most one mana of each colour.
- The Seventh Doctor: not attempted — a hidden card choice plus the defending player's guess.
- The Master, Gallifrey's End, Taster of Wares, The Dragon-Kami Reborn, Sanar, Kylox's Voltstrider, Haldan, Draugr Necromancer, Mavinda: not attempted — free-cast/exile-linked permission sets with counters or per-card conditions.
- Back from the Brink: not attempted — the cost is "pay its mana cost" of the card exiled as part of that cost.
- Dermotaxi: not attempted — an "as it enters" imprint exile of a card from any graveyard and a copy of the linked card.
- Enduring Angel // Angelic Enforcer: not attempted — a replacement of "life total would be reduced to 0 or less" with a conditional transform and lose.
- Deification: not attempted — a replacement of damage to a planeswalker that would remove all loyalty.
- Leori, Sparktouched Hunter: not attempted — copying a planeswalker's loyalty ability for the turn.
- Chaotic Transformation: not attempted — five optional targets, then a reveal-until per exiled permanent's controller with a shared-type match.
- Braided Net // Braided Quipu: not attempted — an activated-ability lock that lasts as long as the permanent is tapped.
- Exterminator Magmarch: not attempted — a copy with a chosen second target among other opponents' permanents.
- Diabolic Servitude: not attempted — links among the creature, the Aura-like enchantment and the returned card.
