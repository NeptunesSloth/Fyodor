# Group permissions to assign damage as though unblocked

Implementation was prepared without imported engine edits during the inventory freeze and integrated after its release: `app/rules/group_unblocked.py`, real Oracle fixture `tests/fixtures/group_unblocked_cards.json`, and gameplay test scaffold `tests/test_rules_group_unblocked.py`.

The shared combat system already handles the choice correctly for a creature with its own permission: `combat.ask_split` asks its controller immediately before assignment; `compute_combat_damage` sends all its damage to what it attacks when accepted, otherwise uses ordinary blocked/trample assignment. Blocking creatures still deal their own damage normally. `assign_unblocked` choices reset after each damage round, so first-strike and regular damage steps can have different answers. Existing replayable decisions and save/load resume mechanisms can be reused unchanged.

A group static must not grant an ability to creatures. Ruxa's [official rulings](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/e07b8142-6a49-46e7-b862-41f89a59b894/rulings) explicitly say neither of its last two abilities gives creatures an ability. Granting a quoted ability would incorrectly remove a vanilla creature from Ruxa's no-abilities filter. Instead, parse a typed `group_assign_unblocked` static with a normal battlefield selector and an optional `source_attacking` gate. Read the derived current source statics and target creature characteristics at the existing assignment question. This also ensures source departure, source control changes, Human status changes, and loss of source abilities before assignment are respected. Indomitable Might can use the same permission with `who: attached`; the host's controller chooses regardless of the Aura's controller.

The [Invasion of Ikoria rulings](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/5d59c8f2-f6af-40a6-8dfe-8cc45bf231ce/rulings) confirm the choice is individual for each non-Human creature, all-or-nothing, and does not prevent damage from blockers. A Human Soldier is a Human and is excluded; adding another creature subtype does not make it non-Human. The [Siege Behemoth ruling](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/46440515-7923-45ba-ad80-052999eaeaac/rulings) likewise confirms none, some, or all creatures can use the permission. Siege Behemoth's condition is live: it must still be attacking when assignment occurs.

Integration: a scoped `scripts._define` hook for the new static, plus replacing the existing self-only `assign_unblocked` check in `combat.ask_split` with `group_unblocked.allows(plan, attacker, derived_characteristics)`. No new engine resume path or rules-state field is needed.

Verified fixture promotions: Invasion of Ikoria // Zilortha, Apex of Ikoria; Siege Behemoth; Indomitable Might. Ruxa remains PARTIAL because its separate +1/+1 anthem for creatures with no abilities is explicitly refused at `statics.py`'s layer dependency safety check. Removing that refusal alone is unsafe: `_hits` currently chooses anthem members before every layer-six granted ability has been applied. Its pure layer-seven power/toughness selector needs a separate correct post-layer-six evaluation, including both keyword and nonkeyword grants.

Sixteen focused gameplay cases cover accept/decline, individual choices among multiple creatures, non-Human filtering, source departure/control changes, Siege attacking gating, ordinary trample when declined, hostile Aura control, and unchanged no-ability status. All sixteen focused cases pass (14 original cases plus two new cases; the extra run also repeats both accept/decline cases with privacy assertions). Additional verified cases cover double strike with different answers in the two damage steps, Humility removing the source's abilities before assignment, and private decision views. Every assignment decision is checked for replay and JSON save/load. The 73 existing wave19a/combat regressions pass. Ruff and whitespace checks pass. Central inventory/type checking is performed by the parent agent.
