Canonical thirteen-card fixture backend/tests/fixtures/dragonhawk_cards.json, all Oracle metadata copied directly from local printings. Oracle trigger: Whenever Dragonhawk enters or attacks, exile the top X cards of your library, where X is the number of creatures you control with power 4 or greater. You may play those cards until your next end step. At the beginning of your next end step, Dragonhawk deals 2 damage to each opponent for each of those cards that are still exiled.

Reuse native selector-count dynamic X for current creatures power>=4 at trigger resolution, including Dragonhawk if still present. Its own death before resolution can reduce X. Native exile_top with as binding captures public new exile OIDs via context.bind_moved; native grant_play who ref binding, play+cast=True, until=next_end_step, from_exile=True. Timing and costs remain normal: land uses land play, creatures obey sorcery timing, spells must pay mana; no freecast. Native permission code already stores granted_step and expiry checks step_serial; same-turn extra end steps supported, including a grant made after original end step began.

Native delayed preserves step_serial. Reusable values.live_ref_count counts context.objects exact live references with an optional zone filter. One native damage instruction uses 2 times that value for each opponent as ONE batch. Native damage replacement/prevention applies, do not directly change life.
