# Agitator Ant: optional counter offers

The official ruling requires public choices in APNAP order before counters are placed. The choice is optional and untargeted, including when a player controls only one eligible creature. Creatures that cannot receive the specified counters are excluded. The native counter replacement planner prepares the whole group with each player's actual putter identity and effect provenance, then commits the counter instruction in one logical event group.

Goad is based on positive actual counter events for the exact chosen objects, not on accepting the offer or comparing counter totals afterwards. Accordingly, two opposing Vorinclex replacements can reduce the counters to zero without goading. Doubling Season correctly affects this resolving effect. Native counter replacement continuations and goad duration machinery handle pauses, replay, source departure, and expiry at the ability controller's next turn.

Twelve canonical cases and 71 existing goad/counter/replacement cases passed (83 unique), including fresh-process offer and replacement resumes. Noble Heritage remains a separate partial card: it can share this offer primitive, but protection from a specified player requires controller/owner provenance through targeting, damage and Aura checks. It is not marked FULL by this change.
