# Wave 111D: researched and refused Star Trek cards

Group D (activated abilities, counters, activation and casting costs) of wave 111. Oracle text is Scryfall's of 2026-10-30 (pre-release); CR 2026-09-25.
One line per card: the exact clause and why it stays PARTIAL.

- **The Guardian of Forever** — "{4}, Sacrifice another nontoken creature: Return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control at the beginning of the next end step.": the effect must find the sacrificed card's new object wherever it went (400.7j) and hand it to a delayed trigger; a sacrificed commander may be in the command zone (903.9a), and no ruling says whether it then returns from there. The Star Trek release notes (2026-11) have no ruling for this card: still unsettled.

Nog, Ambitious Cadet and Queen's Diamond were refused here at first and are now FULL (waves 111R1 and 111R2). Commander Spock is FULL since wave 112T (`app/rules/trek112t.py`): its release-notes rulings settle the mana value ("If the revealed card has {X} in its mana cost, X is 0 for the purpose of determining its mana value"), and "still on top of your library" is the revealed object left unmoved (400.7).
