# Wave 111D: researched and refused Star Trek cards

Group D (activated abilities, counters, activation and casting costs) of wave 111. Oracle text is Scryfall's of 2026-10-30 (pre-release); CR 2026-09-25.
One line per card: the exact clause and why it stays PARTIAL.

- **Commander Spock** — "You may cast that card without paying its mana cost if that spell's mana value is equal to or less than the number of differently named lands you control. If it's a land card, you may put it onto the battlefield tapped. If that card is still on top of your library, draw a card.": when "that spell's mana value" is judged (the card before casting, or the spell during casting with X chosen, 601.2f/601.3e) has no ruling yet, and "still on top of your library" needs the revealed object tracked (400.7); none of the three sentences is read today.
- **The Guardian of Forever** — "{4}, Sacrifice another nontoken creature: Return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control at the beginning of the next end step.": the effect must find the sacrificed card's new object wherever it went (400.7j) and hand it to a delayed trigger; a sacrificed commander may be in the command zone (903.9a), and no ruling says whether it then returns from there. The payment record keeps only the permanents' old objects.

Nog, Ambitious Cadet and Queen's Diamond were refused here at first and are now FULL (waves 111R1 and 111R2).
