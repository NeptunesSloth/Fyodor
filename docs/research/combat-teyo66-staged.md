# Teyo directional restriction — production exact-one admitted

Exact Teyo, Geometric Tactician minus-two grammar delegates activation, loyalty and public direction decisions to native handlers. The direction is frozen in a native continuous effect until the original ability controller's next turn. Declare-only restrictions use current living seat order, intersect with other restrictions and permit only the nearest opponent or their planeswalkers. Other creatures entering attacking bypass declaration restrictions. Permanent Pramikon and Mystic Barrier grammars remain unrecognized; no borrowed-choice acquisition state is reused.
