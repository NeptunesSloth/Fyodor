# Teyo directional restriction — isolated prototype

Exact Teyo, Geometric Tactician minus-two grammar delegates activation, loyalty and public direction decisions to native handlers. The direction is frozen in a native continuous effect until the original ability controller's next turn. Declare-only restrictions use current living seat order, intersect with other restrictions and permit only the nearest opponent or their planeswalkers. Other creatures entering attacking bypass declaration restrictions. Permanent Pramikon and Mystic Barrier grammars remain unrecognized; no borrowed-choice acquisition state is reused.

The isolated prototype is in `backend/app/rules/combat_teyo66.py`; proposed shared hooks are `/tmp/combat66-teyo-kernel-proposal.patch`. No shared Teyo hooks installed and no card counted. Whole-native test draft remains outside live collection in `proposals/combat66-teyo-tests.py`; isolated copy runs under `/tmp/combat66-direction-stage/backend`. Canonical card records, all official rulings and the printed directions semantics are in `/tmp/combat66-static-canonicals.json`, `/tmp/combat66-static-rulings.json` and the separate direction research artifacts.
