# Revealing a Dragon as an optional additional cost

The Dragon reveal family uses canonical Oracle text for Silumgar's Scorn, Orator of Ojutai, Draconic Roar, Dragonlord's Prerogative, Foul-Tongue Invocation, and Scaleguard Sentinels. Each lets its caster reveal a Dragon card from their hand as an optional additional cost. The reveal uses the existing typed `REVEAL_FROM_HAND` payment, native optional-cost announcement, validation, public reveal, and saved payment history. Declining is legal even with a Dragon available (CR 118.8, 601.2b, 601.2h, 701.20).

The shared condition “you revealed a Dragon card or controlled a Dragon as you cast this spell” reads immutable payment facts: the subtypes of cards revealed for the cost, and the subtypes of permanents controlled when casting finishes. It never rechecks the battlefield at resolution. Native characteristics include changeling in every zone. The facts travel with the existing payment record when a spell becomes a permanent and survive save/replay. A source that subsequently leaves or changes controller cannot change whether its caster met the condition.

Existing conditional effects handle Scorn's hard-counter alternative, Roar's damage to the target creature's controller, Orator's intervening-if enters trigger, and Invocation's life rider. Scaleguard's trailing cast condition is normalized to the native conditional entry-counter replacement. Prerogative has a typed conditional self-uncounterability property evaluated against its payment record; it does not resolve an effect to obtain that protection.

Canonical gameplay tests cover the absent/revealed/controlled branches, source leaving before resolution, a Dragon acquired only afterward, changeling on the battlefield and in hand, declined and impossible optional costs, actual counterspell responses, linked creature damage, sacrifice and life gain, entry counters, pending-choice serialization, replay, and save/load. Related evidence/behold/blight suites protect the existing optional-cost infrastructure.
