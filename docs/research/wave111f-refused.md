# Wave 111F refused (Star Trek, group F)

- Marooned: "Enchanted permanent doesn't untap during its controller's untap step and can't be beamed up." — "beamed up" is the new "beam me up" mechanic, which CR 2026-09-25 doesn't define (brief: refuse until the CR has it).
- Kai Winn, Faithless Leader: "… or activates an ability of a source with the chosen name, that player loses 3 life." — the activation includes mana abilities (605.1b) and abilities of objects in any zone ("a source"); the engine reads activation triggers only for abilities "that isn't a mana ability" or loyalty abilities (events._ACTIVATE) and has no selector for "a source" in any zone.
- The Holodeck: "Once during each of your turns, you may play a permanent card you own from outside the game or from your graveyard." — 903.11: traditional cards from outside the game can't be brought into a Commander game except by effects that "specifically bring cards into Commander games"; whether The Holodeck is one is unsettled until its release rulings / CR update (and 903.11a's checks would apply).
