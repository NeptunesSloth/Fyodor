# Wave 111F refused (Star Trek, group F)

- The Holodeck: "Once during each of your turns, you may play a permanent card you own from outside the game or from your graveyard." — 903.11: traditional cards from outside the game can't be brought into a Commander game except by effects that "specifically bring cards into Commander games"; whether The Holodeck is one stays unsettled: its release-notes ruling covers only casual and tournament play ("In a casual game, a card you play from outside the game comes from your personal collection"), not 903.11.

Kai Winn, Faithless Leader and Hugh, First of Many were refused here at first and are now FULL (waves 111R2 and 111R3). Marooned is FULL since wave 112T: "can't be beamed up" is defined by the Star Trek release notes ("A permanent that 'can't be beamed up' can't be returned to a player's hand to pay a beam me up cost"; `app/rules/beam_me_up.py`).
