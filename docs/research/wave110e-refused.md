# Wave 110E refused
- Lethal Throwdown: "the modified creature was sacrificed" is ambiguous between the two cost options; no ruling read.
- Garruk, Curse Breaker (-4): batch "one or more creatures attack" with "those creatures" needs an attack-batch group ref.
- Lunar Hatchling, Celestial Dawn, Tsabo's Web, Arboria: already refused in wave100.
- Head Games, Absorb Identity, Decoy Gambit, Sword-Point Diplomacy, Exotic Pets: multi-player or per-card choice foundations.
- Uphill Battle: "played" means cast for creatures; rewriting to enters-tapped would also tap tokens/reanimated (wrong).
- Absorb Identity: no effect making a group of Shapeshifters become copies.
- Caller of the Hunt, Close Encounter, Sanctuary Blade: a choice made at cast/attach time must persist on the permanent (no cast-time choose-type cost, no last-chosen attach record).
- Piracy: tapping lands you don't control for mana (no foundation). Grip of Amnesia: "exile all cards from graveyard" has no unless-cost form.
- Sanctuary Blade: attach-time color choice would need the equip/attach path to pause for a decision (attachments.attach is synchronous); "last chosen" memory not built.
- Archangel of Strife: per-player war/peace entering choice plus a selector over "players who chose X" not built (one card).
- Maddening Imp, Oracle en-Vec: "each of those creatures that didn't attack" needs the set fixed at resolution (611.2c) and kept for the end-step trigger; no such record yet.
- Uphill Battle: "played" (cast / land play) is not "enters under your opponents' control": tokens and reanimated creatures enter untapped; needs an entered-via-cast fact on the entry plan.
- Necratog: cost "exile the top creature card of your graveyard" is no choice (the most recent one); the cost picker would let the player choose any creature card.
- Teferi's Reproach: protection, life lock and phasing all last "until that player's next turn" for a TARGET player; life_lock/player_shields only track the controller's own turn.
- Head Games: needs a count bound across "puts the cards from hand on top" and a search of another player's library for that many; no such primitive.
- Celestial Dawn: "spend other mana only as though colorless" restricts payment for all mana; payment engine has no per-type conversion limit.
- Ms. Marvel, Kamala Khan: granted static sets base power to a live count (7b, continuous); base-power effects here are read once.
- Arboria: needs a per-player "cast a spell / put a nontoken permanent onto the battlefield last turn" history (previous_own_turns tracks attacks only).
- s7 revisit: Sanctuary Blade (attach-time color choice, last-chosen memory), Radiate (copy per other legal target), Clara Oswald (pre-game color choice), Monitor Monitor (dice rerolls 706.2b), Teferi's Reproach, Ms. Marvel, Celestial Dawn, Arboria still need the foundations named above; not built this session.
- s8: Titania's Song (static "loses all abilities and becomes creature" is no static kind; "continues until end of turn" after leaving needs an LKI-timed effect), Storyweave (one-shot "next time one or more enchantment creatures enter" batch entry replacement; entering_counters is per card), Sanctuary Blade (attach path synchronous, unchanged from s7), Radiate, Selective Adaptation, Bitter Feud, Hawkeye: not built.
- Lethal Throwdown: additional cost 'sacrifice a creature or sacrifice a modified creature' draw-rider ('the modified creature was sacrificed') has no ruling for which option a modified creature sacrificed under the plain option counts as; unsettled.
- s10: built since the notes above: Teferi's Reproach, Sanctuary Blade (equip asks; any other attach path refuses and the player moves it by hand), Monitor Monitor, Maddening Imp, Oracle en-Vec, Ms. Marvel. Still open: Titania's Song (dynamic-vs-locked set after it leaves is not in the CR or the rulings I could reach; needs a layer-4/6 "loses all abilities" static kind), Storyweave, Radiate, Bitter Feud (two-player entering choice plus pair-keyed damage match), Hawkeye, Archangel of Strife, Clara Oswald.
