# Derived-color protection

Six canonical cards now share derived protection qualities: Éowyn, Fearless Knight; Samite Elder; Earnest Fellowship; Empty-Shrine Kannushi; Pledge of Loyalty; and Opal Titan.

The reusable module app/rules/derived_protection.py binds actual colors to the existing protection ability lines. Colorless objects produce no protection quality. Static grants read colors after layer five; controlled-permanent color unions use bounded nested color reads to avoid recursively expanding layer-six grants. Earnest follows each recipient's own current colors. Kannushi and Pledge follow the granting object's controller's current permanents. The enchanted creature's controller does not determine Pledge's colors. Existing keeps=self attachment rules preserve Pledge itself while other matching Auras fall off.

Resolved grants freeze both colors and recipient object identities under CR611.2c. Samite reads the target at resolution, including changes made in response. Éowyn captures the target creature's colors immediately before exile; the card's printed colors in exile do not replace that set. Its abbreviated greater-power target restriction uses the existing source-power comparison, including the resolution recheck. Opal reads the original triggering spell object if still on the stack and otherwise its last-known snapshot; countering that spell does not stop the trigger. Its ordinary animation replaces Enchantment with Creature, preserves the spell's resolved color protection, and retains the native intervening enchantment condition.

Official rulings retrieved from Scryfall during preparation: Éowyn uses colors just before the creature leaves; Samite fixes colors at resolution; Earnest gives a colorless creature no protection; Opal still animates when the triggering spell is countered and gives no protection for a colorless spell. Research files: /tmp/derived-protection-{0..5}-{oracle,rulings}.json. Canonical fixtures: tests/fixtures/derived_color_protection_cards.json. Gameplay/replay/cold-load coverage: tests/test_rules_derived_color_protection.py.

The implementation does not add named-card damage, targeting, blocking, or attachment exceptions. Those use the existing protection engine. No new callback or event-state schema is required; the fresh-process test resumes a pending Éowyn target choice and verifies blue protection after the target reverts to printed green in exile.
