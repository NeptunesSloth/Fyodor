# Mana-source draw riders and terminal payments

A mana ability resolves before the player activates another mana ability or finishes the surrounding payment (CR 605.3b, 601.2g–h). A draw replacement may ask for choices during that resolution. Its continuation must finish the current source, remaining sources, and payment before completing the original spell, ability, or special action. Re-running the original intent after paying would repeat costs; simply opening a decision and continuing the caller would perform later costs too early.

The payment continuation registry serializes typed data and registered stage names, without saving Python callables. Each `pay_then` call has a terminal callback that runs exactly once, synchronously or after a replacement choice. Callers return after handing control to it. Existing replacement bodies retain their native `body_done` continuation, applied-effect tracking and sequential draw processing.

Optional resolution payments save their context, concrete cost, selected objects, source snapshot and pre-payment object references. Their callback pays the nonmana parts and binds the result, then resumes the updated context. Deferred payments carry a remainder queue and the final simultaneous sacrifice batch. They never serialize a second, stale copy of the context for the final resume. Sized and tap-X callers hand off to this same terminal path.

Special-action and hand-activation stages preserve the original source object, characteristics, announced values and ability placeholder. Transmute and cycling discard only after mana-source riders finish, then complete the original stack entry once. Suspend, plot, foretell and companion actions perform their final move and bookkeeping once; turning face up happens afterward, including the existing megamorph counter replacement path. Legacy ward/pay-or-sacrifice resolution branches use the same terminal mana-payment interface.

Pinned CR 605.1a excludes an ability whose cost or effect moves a card to or from a library; replacement effects are ignored when making this classification. Thus a printed “Add mana. Draw a card.” ability is an ordinary activated ability, even without an instant-timing reminder. Canonical Chromatic Sphere and Egg are ordinary activated abilities and cannot be activated during another payment.

The low-level continuation tests print legal synthetic mana abilities, then explicitly request `inject_mana_draw_rider`. The shared helper wraps `casting.mana_abilities` for the exact synthetic name and prepends a structured draw rider below Oracle classification. This deliberately tests suspendable plumbing without making a card eligibility or coverage claim. It preserves later riders to test ordering, and is scoped to individual integration tests. Canonical Abundance, target spells, and special-action cards remain unchanged.

Owned integration tests are `test_rules_optional_payment_continuations.py` (Nihil Spellbomb and Tolarian Terror's ward) and `test_rules_special_payment_continuations.py` (transmute, cycling, suspend, plot, foretell, companion and turning face up). Existing sized-payment, tap-X, deferred-sacrifice, counter, morph and hidden-zone suites check the preserved paths.
